## Summary

Paul's final letter — almost certainly his last words before his execution in Rome. He is in chains, facing death, and some friends have deserted him. He writes to Timothy with urgent pastoral tenderness: come to me, guard what has been entrusted to you, preach the word. The letter's emotional register is unlike any other in the NT — a mentor saying farewell to his spiritual son, pressing the gospel into his hands. Chapter 3 contains the NT's clearest statement on the inspiration and use of Scripture. Chapter 4 closes with Paul's famous valediction: "I have fought the good fight, I have finished the race, I have kept the faith."
