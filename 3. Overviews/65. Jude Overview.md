## Summary

A single urgent chapter from Jude, another brother of Jesus who — like James — came to faith after the resurrection. Jude intended to write about their common salvation but felt compelled to write instead about something more pressing: certain people have "crept in unnoticed" who pervert the grace of God into a license for immorality and deny Christ. Jude reaches into obscure OT narratives — the angels who fell, Sodom, Korah's rebellion, Cain, Balaam — and even cites the non-canonical 1 Enoch and the Assumption of Moses to make his case. The letter closes with one of the NT's most celebrated doxologies: "To him who is able to keep you from stumbling… be glory, majesty, dominion, and authority."
