## Summary

Written to Christians scattered across the northern Roman provinces of Asia Minor who are facing persecution and social exclusion. Peter's strategy is not to minimize suffering but to reframe it: Christ himself suffered, and his suffering leads to glory. Believers are sojourners and aliens in this world, but they belong to a royal priesthood, a holy nation — the true Israel of God. The great christological passage of chapter 2, dense with OT quotations about the cornerstone and the chosen people, is one of the NT's richest affirmations that the church is the eschatological fulfillment of Israel's calling. The letter is saturated with the OT, with a christological key: Christ is the suffering servant, the sacrificial lamb, the precious cornerstone.
