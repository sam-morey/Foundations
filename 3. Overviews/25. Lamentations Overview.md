## Summary
Lamentations is five poems of grief over Jerusalem's destruction — raw, honest, and liturgical. Even in the depths, faith surfaces: "The steadfast love of the Lord never ceases; his mercies never come to an end" (3:22–23). It models how to grieve faithfully, bringing sorrow to God rather than suppressing it.
