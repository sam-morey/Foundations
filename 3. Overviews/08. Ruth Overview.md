# Ruth — Overview

**Author:** Unknown
**Date:** ~10th century BC (events ~12th century BC)
**Genre:** Historical Narrative / Short Story
**Key Themes:** [[6. Themes/Grace & Mercy]], [[6. Themes/Love]], [[6. Themes/Covenant]], [[6. Themes/Israel & The Gentiles]], [[6. Themes/Messiah & Christ]]

## Summary
Ruth is a story of loyal love (*hesed*) and redemption set in the time of the judges. A Moabite widow clings to her Israelite mother-in-law Naomi, and through the kinsman-redeemer Boaz, both women are restored. Ruth the Gentile becomes an ancestor of King David — and of Jesus.

## Notable People
| Person | Role | Why They Matter |
|--------|------|-----------------|
| [[4. People/Ruth\|Ruth]] | Moabite widow who commits to Naomi | Her declaration of loyalty ("Where you go, I will go") is a model of *hesed*; as a Gentile she is grafted into Israel and becomes an ancestor of David and Jesus |
| [[4. People/Naomi\|Naomi]] | Bereaved Israelite woman | Her losses drive the narrative; her restoration — from empty to full — is a portrait of God's redemptive reversal |
| [[4. People/Boaz\|Boaz]] | Kinsman-redeemer | His generous redemption of Ruth and Naomi foreshadows Christ's role as redeemer who pays the cost to restore what was lost |
