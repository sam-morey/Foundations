## Summary
1 Kings follows the reigns from Solomon's golden age through the division of the kingdom. Solomon builds the Temple but his heart is divided. After his death, the kingdom splits into Israel (north) and Judah (south). The prophet Elijah confronts Baal worship under Ahab — the book's moral and theological climax.

## Notable People
| Person | Role | Why They Matter |
|--------|------|-----------------|
| [[4. People/Solomon\|Solomon]] | King of Israel; Temple builder | The wisest king and the one who builds the Temple; his divided heart in old age — foreign wives and idolatry — leads to the kingdom's split, showing that wisdom without faithfulness cannot sustain covenant blessing |
| [[4. People/Elijah\|Elijah]] | Prophet of fire | Confronts Baal worship under Ahab in the book's theological climax (ch. 18); his weakness and renewal at Horeb (ch. 19) model honest faith; the NT identifies him with John the Baptist |
| Ahab | King of northern Israel | The most wicked king so far; his partnership with Jezebel makes Baal worship the official religion of Israel and provides the crisis Elijah addresses |
| Jezebel | Queen of Israel; Baal patroness | Her persecution of the prophets and murder of Naboth illustrate the deadly corruption of covenant land by foreign religious allegiances |
