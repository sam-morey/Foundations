## Summary

A contemporary of Isaiah, Micah prophesied to both Israel and Judah during the reigns of Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah. He comes from a small Judean town and gives voice to the oppressed poor against corrupt rulers, false prophets, and greedy merchants. Yet Micah's message is not only judgment — it swings dramatically toward hope. His most famous oracle pinpoints the birthplace of the coming Messiah: Bethlehem (5:2). His most famous ethical summary distills the entire covenant into three verbs: do justice, love kindness, walk humbly with your God (6:8).
