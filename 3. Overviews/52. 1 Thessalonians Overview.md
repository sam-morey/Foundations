## Summary

One of Paul's earliest letters, written to the young church at Thessalonica shortly after he was forced to leave under persecution. The Thessalonians were facing hardship, and they had questions — and perhaps confusion — about believers who had already died before Christ's return. Would those who died miss the resurrection? Paul assures them: no. When the Lord comes, the dead in Christ will rise first. He then addresses how to live in the present age: in holiness, love, and sober readiness. The letter's tone is warm and parental — Paul compares himself to a nursing mother and an encouraging father.
