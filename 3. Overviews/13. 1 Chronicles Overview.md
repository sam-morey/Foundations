**Author:** "The Chronicler" (traditionally Ezra)
**Date:** ~5th–4th century BC
**Genre:** Historical Narrative
**Key Themes:** [[6. Themes/Covenant]], [[6. Themes/Prayer & Worship]], [[6. Themes/Shepherd & King]]

## Summary
1 Chronicles retells Israel's history from Adam to David, emphasizing the Davidic line, the Temple, and true worship. Written for the post-exilic community, it shows God's faithfulness to the covenant and encourages a new generation to reestablish proper worship.

## Notable People
| Person | Role | Why They Matter |
|--------|------|-----------------|
| [[4. People/David\|David]] | Warrior-king and worship organizer | Chronicles presents David's ideal side — organizing the Levites, preparing for the Temple, leading in worship; his covenant (ch. 17) and prayer of offering (ch. 29) are theological high points |
| [[4. People/Solomon\|Solomon]] | Temple builder | David's plans are handed to Solomon with the charge to build God's house; his role here is to complete what David envisioned, pointing to the One who will be the true Temple |
