## Summary

Written shortly after 1 Thessalonians to correct a new problem: someone — perhaps a forged letter claiming Paul's authority — had told the church that the Day of the Lord had already come. This was causing both panic and a troubling idleness, as some believers had apparently stopped working to wait for the end. Paul addresses both issues. He insists that certain events must precede the Day of the Lord — notably the "man of lawlessness" must be revealed — and that in the meantime, believers must work, not become dependent on others. The mysterious figure of "the restrainer" who holds back lawlessness (2:6–7) has generated enormous theological discussion across the centuries.
