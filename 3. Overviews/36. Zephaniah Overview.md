## Summary

Zephaniah prophesied in the reign of Josiah, just before the great reform movement — a dark period when Baal worship, Assyrian astral cults, and syncretism had deeply infected Judah. His opening is stark: a coming Day of the Lord that will sweep away everything. But the darkness gives way to one of the Old Testament's most remarkable promises. After purifying his people through judgment, God himself will rejoice over his restored remnant "with gladness" and "with loud singing" (3:17) — the only place in Scripture where God is described as singing over his people.
