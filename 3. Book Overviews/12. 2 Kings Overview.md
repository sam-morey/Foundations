# 2 Kings — Overview

**Author:** Unknown
**Date:** ~6th century BC
**Genre:** Historical Narrative
**Key Themes:** [[Judgment]], [[Covenant]], [[Prophecy & Fulfillment]]

## Summary
2 Kings chronicles the decline and fall of both kingdoms — Israel (722 BC, Assyrian exile) and Judah (586 BC, Babylonian exile). Elijah's ministry is continued by Elisha. The book demonstrates that God's covenant warnings (Deuteronomy 28) come true, and ends with Judah in captivity — setting the stage for the exile literature and the hope of return.
