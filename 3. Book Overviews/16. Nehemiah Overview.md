# Nehemiah — Overview

**Author:** Nehemiah (personal memoir sections) / Ezra (traditionally)
**Date:** ~5th century BC
**Genre:** Historical Narrative / Memoir
**Key Themes:** [[Prayer & Worship]], [[Covenant]], [[Grace & Mercy]], [[Justice & Righteousness]]

## Summary
Nehemiah returns to Jerusalem to rebuild the walls, uniting leadership, prayer, and practical action. Despite opposition, the walls are rebuilt in 52 days. The book pairs physical restoration with spiritual renewal — the Law is read publicly, and the covenant is renewed.
