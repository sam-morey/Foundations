# Psalms — Overview

**Author:** Multiple — David (73 attributed), Asaph, Sons of Korah, Solomon, Moses, others
**Date:** ~10th–5th century BC (compiled in stages)
**Genre:** Poetry / Hymns / Prayers
**Key Themes:** [[Prayer & Worship]], [[Suffering & Perseverance]], [[Messiah & Christ]], [[Faith & Trust]], [[Wisdom]]

## Summary
The Psalms are Israel's prayer and songbook — 150 poems that model every form of address to God: praise, lament, confession, thanksgiving, trust, and royal/messianic proclamation. They are quoted more in the NT than any other OT book.

## Structure: Five Books
| Book | Psalms | Flavor |
|------|--------|--------|
| I | 1–41 | Davidic; individual lament and trust |
| II | 42–72 | Davidic + Korahite; national themes |
| III | 73–89 | Asaphite; national lament (exile) |
| IV | 90–106 | Enthronement psalms; God's sovereignty |
| V | 107–150 | Hallel, pilgrimage, praise; ends with pure praise |
