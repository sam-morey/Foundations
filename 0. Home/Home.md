## The Bible's Story in Four Movements

The Bible is not a random collection of texts. It tells one unified story:

| Movement | Content | Key Books |
|----------|---------|-----------|
| **Creation & Fall** | God makes a good world; humanity rebels | Genesis 1–11 |
| **Covenant & Promise** | God calls Abraham; the long story of Israel | Genesis 12 – Malachi |
| **Fulfillment in Christ** | Jesus arrives; the Kingdom breaks in | Matthew – John |
| **The Spirit & the Church** | The mission spreads; the New Creation arrives | Acts – Revelation |
