# Messiah & Christ

*Messiah* (Hebrew) and *Christ* (Greek) both mean "Anointed One." The OT builds a detailed portrait of a coming king, priest, and prophet — and the NT proclaims that Jesus of Nazareth is that person.

- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/01. Genesis/Genesis 3\|Genesis 3:15]] — The seed of the woman will crush the serpent (*protevangelium*)
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/01. Genesis/Genesis 12\|Genesis 12]], [[2. The Bible (ESV)/01. Genesis/Genesis 22\|Genesis 22]] — Through Abraham's offspring, all nations blessed
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/04. Numbers/Numbers 24\|Numbers 24]] — Balaam's oracle: "A star shall come out of Jacob"
