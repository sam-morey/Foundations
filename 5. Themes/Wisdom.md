# Wisdom

Biblical wisdom is not mere cleverness or intellectual knowledge — it is *skill for living*, grounded in the fear of the Lord. The Wisdom literature (Job, Psalms, Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, Song of Solomon) and the NT epistles explore how to live well before God and others.

## Key Passages

### The Song of Solomon
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/22. Song of Solomon/Song of Solomon 1\|Song of Solomon]] — Wisdom includes understanding the goodness of human love, beauty, and intimacy

- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40. Matthew/Matthew 11\|Matthew 11]] — "Wisdom is justified by her deeds" (Jesus as wisdom incarnate)
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/46. I Corinthians/I Corinthians 1\|1 Corinthians 1–2]] — "Christ the power of God and the wisdom of God"; God's wisdom appears foolish to the world

- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/59. James/James 3\|James 3]] — Wisdom from above: pure, peaceable, gentle, full of mercy

## The Fear of the Lord
The fear of the Lord (*yir'at YHWH*) is not terror but reverent awe — recognizing who God is and ordering your life accordingly. It is the moral and spiritual foundation of all genuine wisdom.
