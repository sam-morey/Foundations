# Faith & Trust

Faith is trusting God — his character, his word, and his promises — even when circumstances suggest otherwise. The Bible presents faith not as blind optimism but as a reasoned confidence grounded in who God is and what he has done.

## Faith vs. Works

A key tension in Scripture:
- Paul insists justification is by faith alone, not works: [[2. The Bible (ESV)/45. Romans/Romans 3\|Romans 3]], [[2. The Bible (ESV)/48. Galatians/Galatians 2\|Galatians 2]]
- James insists genuine faith produces works: [[2. The Bible (ESV)/59. James/James 2\|James 2]]

These are not contradictory — both describe the same reality from different angles. Paul argues against *earning* salvation; James argues against a mere *intellectual* assent that changes nothing.
