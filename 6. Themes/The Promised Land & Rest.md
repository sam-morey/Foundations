God promised Abraham a land — a place of security, abundance, and his presence. This promise shapes Israel's entire story. In the NT, the physical land becomes a type of a greater rest: life in the new creation.

## Key Passages

### The Promise of Land
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/01. Genesis/Genesis 12\|Genesis 12]] — "To your offspring I will give this land"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/01. Genesis/Genesis 15\|Genesis 15]] — The covenant ceremony; land boundaries defined
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/01. Genesis/Genesis 17\|Genesis 17]] — "I will give to you and to your offspring after you the land of Canaan"

### Wilderness and Waiting
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/02. Exodus/Exodus 16\|Exodus 16]] — God provides manna in the wilderness
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/04. Numbers/Numbers 13\|Numbers 13–14]] — The twelve spies; failure to enter due to unbelief; forty years of wandering
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/05. Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 8\|Deuteronomy 8]] — The wilderness as a place of testing and trust

### Entering the Land
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/06. Joshua/Joshua 1\|Joshua 1]] — "Be strong and courageous… I will give you every place where you set your foot"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/06. Joshua/Joshua 3\|Joshua 3]] — Crossing the Jordan; the new generation enters
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/06. Joshua/Joshua 21\|Joshua 21]] — "Not one word of all the good promises that the Lord had made to the house of Israel had failed"

### Exile and the Loss of Land
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/12. II Kings/II Kings 17\|2 Kings 17]] — Israel exiled by Assyria
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/12. II Kings/II Kings 25\|2 Kings 25]] — Judah exiled by Babylon

### The Sabbath as Rest
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/01. Genesis/Genesis 2\|Genesis 2]] — God rests on the seventh day
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/02. Exodus/Exodus 20\|Exodus 20]] — The Sabbath command: "Remember the Sabbath day, to keep it holy"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/05. Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 5\|Deuteronomy 5]] — Sabbath as rest for all, including servants

### The Greater Rest (New Testament)
- [[Psalms 95\|Psalm 95]] — "Today, if you hear his voice, do not harden your hearts" — entering God's rest
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40. Matthew/Matthew 11\|Matthew 11]] — "Come to me, all who are weary… and I will give you rest"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/58. Hebrews/Hebrews 3\|Hebrews 3–4]] — The wilderness generation failed to enter rest; a Sabbath rest remains for God's people; enter by faith today
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/66. Revelation of John/Revelation of John 21\|Revelation 21]] — The new creation as the final land/home
