# History & Archaeology — Index

The Bible was written in real places by real people living through real historical events. This section connects Scripture to the broader sweep of ancient world history and to the archaeological discoveries that illuminate, confirm, and contextualize the biblical narrative.

## Key Archaeological Artifacts — Quick Reference

| Artifact | Date | Held At | Biblical Connection |
|----------|------|---------|---------------------|
| Merneptah Stele | ~1208 BC | Cairo Museum | Earliest extra-biblical mention of Israel |
| Mesha Stele (Moabite Stone) | ~840 BC | Louvre, Paris | Mentions "House of David"; confirms Omri dynasty |
| Tel Dan Stele | ~840 BC | Israel Museum, Jerusalem | "House of David" — first epigraphic proof of the Davidic dynasty |
| Black Obelisk of Shalmaneser III | ~825 BC | British Museum, London | Depicts Jehu of Israel bowing to pay tribute |
| Siloam Tunnel Inscription | ~701 BC | Istanbul Archaeological Museum | Hezekiah's water tunnel (2 Kings 20:20) |
| Sennacherib's Prism (Taylor Prism) | ~691 BC | British Museum, London | Siege of Jerusalem; Hezekiah shut up "like a bird in a cage" |
| Lachish Reliefs | ~700 BC | British Museum, London | Assyrian siege of Lachish depicted in stone (2 Kings 18–19) |
| Cyrus Cylinder | ~539 BC | British Museum, London | Cyrus's policy of returning exiles and restoring temples |
| Elephantine Papyri | 5th c. BC | Various museums | Jewish military colony in Egypt; correspondence with Jerusalem |
| Great Isaiah Scroll (1QIsa-a) | ~125 BC | Israel Museum (Shrine of the Book) | Complete Isaiah scroll; 1,000 years older than previous copies |
| Pilate Stone | 1st c. AD | Israel Museum, Jerusalem | Only inscription ever found naming Pontius Pilate as prefect |
| Gallio Inscription | ~51–52 AD | Delphi Museum | Dates Paul's time in Corinth to 51–52 AD with precision |
| Ossuary of Caiaphas | 1st c. AD | Israel Museum, Jerusalem | Burial box of the high priest who presided at Jesus's trial |
