# Creation & New Creation

God creates, sustains, and ultimately renews all things. Scripture opens with God as the sovereign Creator and closes with the promise of a new heaven and new earth — bookending the entire biblical story.

## Key Passages

### Creation
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/01. Genesis/Genesis 1\|Genesis 1]] — God creates the heavens and the earth in six days; humanity made in his image
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/01. Genesis/Genesis 2\|Genesis 2]] — Detailed account of humanity's formation and the Garden of Eden
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/19. Psalms/Psalms 19\|Psalm 19]] — The heavens declare God's glory
- [[Psalms 104\|Psalm 104]] — Hymn to God as sustainer of creation
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/20. Proverbs/Proverbs 8\|Proverbs 8]] — Wisdom present at creation
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/43. John/John 1\|John 1]] — The Word (Logos) was the agent of all creation
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/51. Colossians/Colossians 1\|Colossians 1]] — All things created through and for Christ
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/58. Hebrews/Hebrews 1\|Hebrews 1]] — God created the ages through the Son

### Creation Corrupted (The Fall)
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/01. Genesis/Genesis 3\|Genesis 3]] — The Fall; creation cursed
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/45. Romans/Romans 8\|Romans 8]] — Creation groans, waiting for redemption

### New Creation
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/23. Isaiah/Isaiah 65\|Isaiah 65]] — God promises a new heavens and new earth
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/23. Isaiah/Isaiah 66\|Isaiah 66]] — The new creation described
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/47. II Corinthians/II Corinthians 5\|2 Corinthians 5]] — Anyone in Christ is a new creation
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/66. Revelation of John/Revelation of John 21\|Revelation 21]] — New heaven, new earth, new Jerusalem
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/66. Revelation of John/Revelation of John 22\|Revelation 22]] — The Garden restored; the Tree of Life

## Development Across Scripture

The Bible moves from **creation → corruption → new creation**:
1. God creates a good world ([[2. The Bible (ESV)/01. Genesis/Genesis 1\|Gen 1–2]])
2. Humanity rebels, bringing corruption ([[2. The Bible (ESV)/01. Genesis/Genesis 3\|Gen 3]])
3. God begins his redemptive project through Israel
4. Christ comes as the second Adam to undo the Fall ([[2. The Bible (ESV)/45. Romans/Romans 5\|Romans 5]])
5. The Spirit is the down payment of the new creation ([[2. The Bible (ESV)/47. II Corinthians/II Corinthians 5\|2 Cor 5]], [[2. The Bible (ESV)/49. Ephesians/Ephesians 1\|Eph 1]])
6. The consummation brings full renewal ([[2. The Bible (ESV)/66. Revelation of John/Revelation of John 21\|Rev 21–22]])
