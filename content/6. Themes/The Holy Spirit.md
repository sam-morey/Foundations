# The Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit is the third person of the Trinity — fully God, personally active in creation, in Israel's life, in Christ's ministry, and in the Church. The Spirit is the divine presence that empowers, sanctifies, and leads God's people.

## Key Passages

### The Spirit in Creation & the Old Testament
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/01. Genesis/Genesis 1\|Genesis 1]] — The Spirit of God hovers over the face of the waters
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/02. Exodus/Exodus 31\|Exodus 31]] — The Spirit fills Bezalel with skill for building the Tabernacle
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/04. Numbers/Numbers 11\|Numbers 11]] — Moses' spirit shared with seventy elders
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/09. I Samuel/I Samuel 16\|1 Samuel 16]] — The Spirit comes mightily upon David at his anointing
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/19. Psalms/Psalms 51\|Psalm 51]] — "Do not take your Holy Spirit from me"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/23. Isaiah/Isaiah 61\|Isaiah 61]] — "The Spirit of the Lord God is upon me" (Jesus quotes this in Luke 4)
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/26. Ezekiel/Ezekiel 36\|Ezekiel 36]] — God will put a new spirit within his people
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/26. Ezekiel/Ezekiel 37\|Ezekiel 37]] — The Spirit breathes life into dry bones
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/29. Joel/Joel 2\|Joel 2]] — "I will pour out my Spirit on all flesh; your sons and daughters will prophesy"

### The Spirit in Jesus' Ministry
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/42. Luke/Luke 1\|Luke 1]] — The Spirit conceives Jesus in Mary; fills Elizabeth and Zechariah
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40. Matthew/Matthew 3\|Matthew 3]] — The Spirit descends like a dove at Jesus' baptism
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/42. Luke/Luke 4\|Luke 4]] — Jesus anointed with the Spirit; reads Isaiah 61
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/43. John/John 3\|John 3]] — "Unless one is born of water and the Spirit, he cannot enter the kingdom of God"

### The Spirit Promised and Given
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/43. John/John 14\|John 14]] — Jesus promises the Helper (Paraclete/Advocate)
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/43. John/John 15\|John 15]] — The Spirit of truth will testify about Jesus
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/43. John/John 16\|John 16]] — The Spirit will convict the world and guide into all truth
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/43. John/John 20\|John 20]] — Jesus breathes on the disciples: "Receive the Holy Spirit"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/44. Acts/Acts 1\|Acts 1]] — Jesus promises power when the Spirit comes
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/44. Acts/Acts 2\|Acts 2]] — Pentecost: the Spirit poured out; Joel 2 fulfilled; 3,000 saved

- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/45. Romans/Romans 8\|Romans 8]] — The Spirit gives life, leads God's children, intercedes in prayer, and is the down-payment of resurrection
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/46. I Corinthians/I Corinthians 12\|1 Corinthians 12]] — Spiritual gifts: "All these are empowered by one and the same Spirit"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/48. Galatians/Galatians 5\|Galatians 5]] — The fruit of the Spirit vs. the works of the flesh

- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/49. Ephesians/Ephesians 5\|Ephesians 5]] — "Be filled with the Spirit"
