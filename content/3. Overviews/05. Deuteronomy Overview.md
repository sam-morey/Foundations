## Summary
Deuteronomy ("second law") is Moses' farewell address to the second generation of Israel on the plains of Moab before they enter the Promised Land. It renews the Sinai covenant, emphasizes covenant loyalty and love for God, and sets before the nation the choice of life or death. Jesus quotes Deuteronomy three times resisting temptation.
