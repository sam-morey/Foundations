## Summary

The second shortest book in the New Testament (13 verses), 2 John is a personal letter from "the elder" — John — to "the elect lady and her children," almost certainly a metaphor for a local church. The letter has two interlocking concerns: walk in love, and do not extend hospitality to traveling teachers who deny that Jesus Christ came in the flesh. In John's world, itinerant teachers depended on the hospitality of house churches; welcoming a false teacher was effectively funding and endorsing their mission. The same Spirit that commands radical love also commands careful discernment — the two are not in tension but inseparable.
