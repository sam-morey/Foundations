**Author:** "Qohelet" (the Preacher/Teacher) — traditionally Solomon
**Date:** ~10th or ~4th–3rd century BC (debated)
**Genre:** Wisdom / Philosophy
**Key Themes:** [[6. Themes/Wisdom]], [[6. Themes/Suffering & Perseverance]], [[6. Themes/Faith & Trust]]

## Summary
Ecclesiastes is the Bible's most searching examination of the meaning of life "under the sun." The Preacher (Qohelet) explores wisdom, pleasure, wealth, work, and death — and finds them all *hebel* ("vanity/vapor/breath"). The conclusion: fear God and keep his commandments, for life lived apart from him is ultimately empty.
