**Author:** Solomon (primarily), Agur, Lemuel, others
**Date:** ~10th–6th century BC (collected and edited)
**Genre:** Wisdom / Poetry
**Key Themes:** [[6. Themes/Wisdom]], [[6. Themes/Justice & Righteousness]], [[6. Themes/Love]]

## Summary
Proverbs is a collection of wise sayings for living well before God and others. It frames all wisdom within "the fear of the Lord." Lady Wisdom calls all to her table; the foolish path leads to death. The book is practical, earthy, and deeply theological.
