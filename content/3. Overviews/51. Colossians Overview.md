# Colossians — Overview

**Author:** Paul | **Date:** ~60–62 AD | **Genre:** Epistle (Prison Letter) | **Key Themes:** [[6. Themes/Messiah & Christ|Messiah & Christ]] · [[6. Themes/Wisdom|Wisdom]] · [[6. Themes/Creation & New Creation|Creation & New Creation]]

---

## Summary

Written to a church Paul had not personally founded, in a region where a dangerous philosophy was circulating — likely proto-Gnostic, requiring special rituals, angel veneration, and ascetic practices as the path to spiritual fullness. Paul's answer is simple and total: Christ is enough. He is the image of the invisible God, the firstborn over all creation, the one in whom all the fullness of deity dwells bodily. Every spiritual treasure is hidden in him. The practical sections of the letter apply this cosmic Christology to daily life: households, work, relationships — all reoriented around Christ as Lord.
