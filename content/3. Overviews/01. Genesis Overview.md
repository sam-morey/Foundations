## Summary
Genesis ("beginnings") records the creation of the world, the entrance of sin, and the calling of Abraham and his family through whom God will bless all nations. It ends with Israel in Egypt, setting the stage for Exodus.

## Notable People
| Person | Role | Why They Matter |
|--------|------|-----------------|
| [[4. People/Adam\|Adam]] | First man | Created in God's image; his disobedience introduces sin and death into the world, making the need for a second Adam (Christ) central to Scripture |
| [[4. People/Eve\|Eve]] | First woman | Partner in the Fall; the mother of all living; the promise of Gen 3:15 (enmity between her seed and the serpent) is the first messianic prophecy |
| [[4. People/Noah\|Noah]] | Righteous man in a corrupt age | His obedience in building the ark preserves humanity; God's covenant with him (the rainbow) is the first post-Fall covenant with humanity |
| [[4. People/Abraham\|Abraham]] | Father of the covenant people | Called from Ur; justified by faith (Gen 15); father of both Israel and — through faith — all believers; his willingness to sacrifice Isaac foreshadows the cross |
| [[4. People/Sarah\|Sarah]] | Wife of Abraham; mother of Isaac | Her miraculous pregnancy in old age demonstrates that the covenant line depends on God's power, not human strength |
| [[4. People/Isaac\|Isaac]] | Son of the promise | The child God provided in place of Abraham's sacrifice; he carries the covenant promise into the next generation |
| [[4. People/Rebekah\|Rebekah]] | Wife of Isaac; mother of Jacob and Esau | Her initiative shapes the covenant succession; she is the hinge between the Abraham and Jacob narratives |
| [[4. People/Jacob\|Jacob]] | Patriarch of the twelve tribes | His wrestling with God (Gen 32) and name-change to Israel make him the eponymous ancestor of the nation; his twelve sons become the twelve tribes |
| [[4. People/Rachel\|Rachel]] | Beloved wife of Jacob; mother of Joseph and Benjamin | Her death in childbirth and Joseph's birth connect the patriarchal and Joseph narratives |
| [[4. People/Joseph\|Joseph]] | Son of Jacob; savior of the family | Sold into slavery by his brothers, raised to Pharaoh's right hand; his declaration "You meant evil; God meant it for good" (50:20) is the book's theological climax and a type of Christ |
