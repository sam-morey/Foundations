# Obadiah — Overview

**Author:** Obadiah | **Date:** ~586 BC | **Genre:** Prophetic | **Key Themes:** [[6. Themes/Judgment|Judgment]]

---

## Summary

The shortest book in the Old Testament — just 21 verses — Obadiah delivers a single laser-focused oracle against Edom, the nation descended from Esau. When Babylon sacked Jerusalem in 586 BC, Edom stood by and gloated, cutting off fleeing refugees and even looting the city. Obadiah declares that this betrayal of a brother nation will not go unpunished. The Day of the Lord is coming on all nations, and Edom's arrogant confidence in its mountain strongholds will be undone. The book ends with a vision of restored Israel and the Lord's kingdom.
