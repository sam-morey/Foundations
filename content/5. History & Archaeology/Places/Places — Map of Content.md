Biblical places are the geography of redemption — each location grounds Scripture in physical, datable reality. These notes cover locations mentioned in the Bible with focus on their biblical narrative significance, historical background, and archaeological confirmation.

---

## The Ancient Near East

The world of the patriarchs — the broad sweep of Mesopotamia, Canaan, and Egypt from which Israel emerged.

- [[Ur of the Chaldees]] — Abraham's homeland in southern Mesopotamia; the Royal Tombs of Ur; civilization at its height before God calls one man out
- [[Hebron]] — The patriarchal heartland; Abraham at Mamre; the Cave of Machpelah; David's first capital before Jerusalem
- [[Babylon]] — The great empire of exile; the Ishtar Gate; Nebuchadnezzar; Daniel and the captivity
- [[Nineveh]] — Assyrian capital; Jonah's reluctant mission; Sennacherib's palace; Nahum's prophecy of its fall

---

## The Exodus & Wilderness

The terrain of Israel's formation as a people — desert, mountain, and covenant.

- [[Mount Sinai (Horeb)]] — The mountain of the Law; the Ten Commandments; the burning bush; Elijah's second calling; Codex Sinaiticus discovered at St. Catherine's Monastery

---

## The Promised Land — Old Testament

The cities and landscapes of Israel's inheritance — from conquest to kingdom to exile.

- [[Jericho]] — The first conquest city; the oldest continuously inhabited city; Rahab's faith and the trumpets; Elisha's healing of the spring
- [[Hazor]] — The largest Canaanite city in the Levant; Joshua's decisive northern victory; Yigael Yadin's excavations
- [[Megiddo]] — The strategic Via Maris crossroads; Solomon's chariot city; future site of Armageddon
- [[Jerusalem]] — David's capital; Solomon's Temple; the city of the crucifixion and resurrection; the New Jerusalem
- [[Caesarea Maritima]] — The Roman administrative capital; the Pilate Stone; Cornelius's conversion; Paul's two-year imprisonment

---

## The Life of Jesus

The geography of the Incarnation — from birth to baptism, from Galilee to Jerusalem and the empty tomb.

- [[Bethlehem]] — Birthplace of David and Jesus; the shepherd fields; the Church of the Nativity; Micah's prophecy
- [[Nazareth]] — Jesus's hometown for thirty years; the synagogue rejection; the first-century house confirmed by archaeology
- [[Jordan River]] — Israel's boundary into the Promised Land; Naaman healed; John the Baptist's ministry; the baptism of Jesus
- [[Sea of Galilee]] — The center of Jesus's Galilean ministry; the fishermen-disciples called; the storm stilled; the walking on water; the 1st-century "Jesus Boat" discovered 1986
- [[Capernaum]] — Jesus's ministry headquarters; Peter's house; the synagogue where he taught and healed
- [[Bethany]] — The village of Mary, Martha, and Lazarus; the raising of Lazarus; Mary's anointing with nard; the Ascension nearby
- [[Garden of Gethsemane]] — The olive garden of Jesus's agonized prayer; the arrest; the Church of All Nations built over the rock of prayer
- [[Mount of Olives]] — The ridge of prophecy and eschatology; the Olivet Discourse; the triumphal entry; the Ascension; Zechariah 14:4
- [[Golgotha & Calvary]] — The site of the crucifixion and resurrection; the Church of the Holy Sepulchre and the archaeological case for its authenticity

---

## The Pauline World

The cities of the early church — from Jerusalem to Rome, along the Roman roads of the first century.

- [[Corinth]] — Paul's 18-month ministry; the Gallio trial; 1 & 2 Corinthians; the Bema seat; the Erastus inscription
- [[Ephesus]] — Paul's three-year base; Diana of the Ephesians; the Artemision; one of Revelation's seven churches
- [[Rome]] — Paul's imprisonment and martyrdom; the center of the empire; the church that received his greatest letter
