# 1 Chronicles — Overview

**Author:** "The Chronicler" (traditionally Ezra)
**Date:** ~5th–4th century BC
**Genre:** Historical Narrative
**Key Themes:** [[Covenant]], [[Prayer & Worship]], [[Shepherd & King]]

## Summary
1 Chronicles retells Israel's history from Adam to David, emphasizing the Davidic line, the Temple, and true worship. Written for the post-exilic community, it shows God's faithfulness to the covenant and encourages a new generation to reestablish proper worship.
