# Daniel — Overview

**Author:** Daniel
**Date:** 6th century BC (events); literary composition debated
**Genre:** Historical Narrative + Apocalyptic
**Key Themes:** [[Kingdom of God]], [[Prophecy & Fulfillment]], [[Faith & Trust]], [[Messiah & Christ]], [[Judgment]]

## Summary
Daniel lives in Babylon under Nebuchadnezzar and successive kings, maintaining faithful devotion to God under persecution. The second half (chs. 7–12) contains visions of world empires and the coming Kingdom of God — including the "Son of Man" figure (ch. 7) who receives an eternal kingdom. Jesus frequently echoes Daniel's imagery.
