# Mark — Overview

**Author:** John Mark (companion of Peter)
**Date:** ~50–60 AD
**Genre:** Gospel
**Key Themes:** [[Messiah & Christ]], [[Kingdom of God]], [[Sacrifice & Atonement]], [[Suffering & Perseverance]]

## Summary
Mark is the shortest and most urgent Gospel — full of action ("immediately," "at once," "straightway"). It presents Jesus as the powerful Son of God who serves and gives his life as a ransom. The "Messianic Secret" (Jesus repeatedly silences those who recognize him) runs throughout. The climax is the centurion's confession at the cross.
