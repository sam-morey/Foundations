# Luke — Overview

**Author:** Luke, a physician and companion of Paul
**Date:** ~60–70 AD
**Genre:** Gospel
**Key Themes:** [[Redemption & Salvation]], [[Israel & The Gentiles]], [[Grace & Mercy]], [[The Holy Spirit]], [[Prayer & Worship]], [[Justice & Righteousness]]

## Summary
Luke presents Jesus as the Savior of the world — with special attention to the poor, the outsider, women, Samaritans, and Gentiles. The Holy Spirit is prominent from birth narratives through Pentecost (see Acts). Luke's Jesus prays constantly and has compassion for those on the margins. Luke–Acts forms a two-part work on the Spirit-empowered advance of God's Kingdom.
