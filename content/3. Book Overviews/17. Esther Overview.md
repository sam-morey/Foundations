# Esther — Overview

**Author:** Unknown
**Date:** ~5th–4th century BC
**Genre:** Historical Narrative
**Key Themes:** [[Grace & Mercy]], [[Judgment]], [[Israel & The Gentiles]]

## Summary
Esther tells how a Jewish woman becomes queen of Persia and, through her courage and the wisdom of her cousin Mordecai, saves the Jewish people from genocide. God is never named in the book, yet his providence is visible throughout. The events give rise to the festival of Purim.
