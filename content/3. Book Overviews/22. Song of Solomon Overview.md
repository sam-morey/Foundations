# Song of Solomon — Overview

**Author:** Solomon (attributed)
**Date:** ~10th century BC
**Genre:** Wisdom / Love Poetry
**Key Themes:** [[Love]], [[Wisdom]]

## Summary
The Song of Solomon (Song of Songs) is a collection of love poems between a man and a woman, celebrating the goodness of human love, desire, beauty, and intimacy. In the Jewish and Christian tradition it has also been read as an allegory of God's love for Israel / Christ's love for the Church. It belongs in the Wisdom literature as a meditation on one of life's deepest goods.
