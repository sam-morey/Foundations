# Genesis — Overview

**Author:** Moses (traditional attribution)
**Date:** ~15th–13th century BC
**Genre:** Narrative / Origins
**Key Themes:** [[Covenant]], [[Creation & New Creation]], [[Sin & The Fall]], [[Messiah & Christ]], [[The Promised Land & Rest]]

## Summary
Genesis ("beginnings") records the creation of the world, the entrance of sin, and the calling of Abraham and his family through whom God will bless all nations. It ends with Israel in Egypt, setting the stage for Exodus.

| Section | Chapters | Content |

| Joseph | 37–50 | Betrayal, Egypt, providence |
