# Ruth — Overview

**Author:** Unknown
**Date:** ~10th century BC (events ~12th century BC)
**Genre:** Historical Narrative / Short Story
**Key Themes:** [[Grace & Mercy]], [[Love]], [[Covenant]], [[Israel & The Gentiles]], [[Messiah & Christ]]

## Summary
Ruth is a story of loyal love (*hesed*) and redemption set in the time of the judges. A Moabite widow clings to her Israelite mother-in-law Naomi, and through the kinsman-redeemer Boaz, both women are restored. Ruth the Gentile becomes an ancestor of King David — and of Jesus.
