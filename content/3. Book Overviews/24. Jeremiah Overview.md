# Jeremiah — Overview

**Author:** Jeremiah son of Hilkiah; dictated to Baruch
**Date:** ~627–587 BC (ministry during the fall of Jerusalem)
**Genre:** Prophecy / Narrative / Lament
**Key Themes:** [[Covenant]], [[Judgment]], [[Prophecy & Fulfillment]], [[Suffering & Perseverance]], [[Grace & Mercy]]

## Summary
Jeremiah preached for 40 years to a Jerusalem that would not listen, warning of the coming Babylonian judgment. He is the "weeping prophet," deeply personal in his anguish. Yet he also proclaims the greatest covenant hope in the OT — the New Covenant (ch. 31), where God writes his law on hearts.
