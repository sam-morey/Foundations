# Paul's Letters — Overview

Paul wrote thirteen epistles (Romans through Philemon). See the dedicated [[45. Romans Overview|Romans Overview]] for the fullest treatment. This note covers the remaining twelve.

---

## 1 Corinthians (16 chapters)
**Date:** ~54–55 AD | **Themes:** [[Wisdom]], [[Resurrection & Eternal Life]], [[Love]], [[The Holy Spirit]]

Written to a divided, worldly church in Corinth. Paul addresses factions, lawsuits, sexual immorality, food offered to idols, spiritual gifts, and the resurrection.

Key chapters:
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/46 I Corinthians/Chapter_01\|Ch. 1–2]] — "God's foolishness is wiser than human wisdom"; Christ crucified
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/46 I Corinthians/Chapter_11\|Ch. 11]] — The Lord's Supper
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/46 I Corinthians/Chapter_12\|Ch. 12]] — One body, many gifts; the Spirit distributes as he wills
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/46 I Corinthians/Chapter_13\|Ch. 13]] — "Love is patient, love is kind…" — the greatest gift
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/46 I Corinthians/Chapter_15\|Ch. 15]] — The resurrection: "Death is swallowed up in victory"

Cross-references: [[2. The Bible (ESV)/23 Isaiah/Chapter_25\|Isaiah 25:8]] (death swallowed up), [[2. The Bible (ESV)/28 Hosea/Chapter_13\|Hosea 13:14]] (where is your sting?)

## 2 Corinthians (13 chapters)
**Date:** ~56 AD | **Themes:** [[Suffering & Perseverance]], [[Grace & Mercy]], [[Redemption & Salvation]]

The most personal of Paul's letters; defends his apostleship and describes the paradox of strength in weakness.
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/47 II Corinthians/Chapter_04\|Ch. 4]] — "We have this treasure in jars of clay"; "light and momentary troubles"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/47 II Corinthians/Chapter_05\|Ch. 5]] — New creation; the ministry of reconciliation; God made him sin for us
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/47 II Corinthians/Chapter_12\|Ch. 12]] — "My grace is sufficient… power is made perfect in weakness"

## Galatians (6 chapters)
**Date:** ~48–55 AD | **Themes:** [[Faith & Trust]], [[Grace & Mercy]], [[Law & Torah]], [[Covenant]], [[The Holy Spirit]]

"The Magna Carta of Christian liberty." Paul defends justification by faith alone against those requiring circumcision.
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/48 Galatians/Chapter_02\|Ch. 2]] — "Justified by faith in Christ, not by works of the law"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/48 Galatians/Chapter_03\|Ch. 3]] — Abraham believed; the law was a guardian until Christ; all one in Christ
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/48 Galatians/Chapter_05\|Ch. 5]] — The fruit of the Spirit; freedom vs. license

## Philippians (4 chapters)
**Date:** ~61–62 AD | **Themes:** [[Faith & Trust]], [[Suffering & Perseverance]], [[Prayer & Worship]]

The "Joy Letter" — written from prison, yet saturated with joy. The Christ hymn (ch. 2) is one of the NT's greatest passages.
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/50 Philippians/Chapter_02\|Ch. 2]] — The mind of Christ; "He humbled himself… God exalted him"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/50 Philippians/Chapter_04\|Ch. 4]] — "I can do all things through Christ"; "the peace of God which surpasses understanding"

## Colossians (4 chapters)
**Date:** ~60–62 AD | **Themes:** [[Messiah & Christ]], [[Wisdom]], [[Creation & New Creation]]

- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/51 Colossians/Chapter_01\|Ch. 1]] — "He is the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of all creation"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/51 Colossians/Chapter_02\|Ch. 2]] — In Christ "all treasures of wisdom and knowledge are hidden"

## Philemon (1 chapter)
**Date:** ~60–62 AD | **Theme:** [[Grace & Mercy]], [[Love]]

A personal letter requesting that Philemon receive back his runaway slave Onesimus as "a dear brother." A miniature picture of the gospel: Paul intercedes, offering to pay any debt.
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/57 Philemon/Chapter_01\|Ch. 1]] — Paul's intercession for Onesimus
