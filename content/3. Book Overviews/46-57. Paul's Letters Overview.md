# Paul's Letters — Overview

Paul wrote thirteen epistles (Romans through Philemon). See the dedicated [[content/3. Book Overviews/45. Romans Overview|Romans Overview]] for the fullest treatment. This note covers the remaining twelve.

## Notable People
| Person | Role | Why They Matter |
|--------|------|-----------------|
| [[4. People/Paul\|Paul]] | Author of all 12 letters | The former Pharisee and persecutor of the church became the NT's most prolific writer; his letters shaped Christian theology from Corinth to Colossae to Rome |
| [[4. People/Lydia\|Lydia]] | Notable convert in the Philippian community | The first European believer, whose household formed the core of the Philippian church — the community Paul calls his greatest joy and to which he writes the "Joy Letter" |

## 1 Corinthians (16 chapters)
**Date:** ~54–55 AD | **Themes:** [[6. Themes/Wisdom]], [[6. Themes/Resurrection & Eternal Life]], [[6. Themes/Love]], [[6. Themes/The Holy Spirit]]

Written to a divided, worldly church in Corinth. Paul addresses factions, lawsuits, sexual immorality, food offered to idols, spiritual gifts, and the resurrection.

Key chapters:
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/46. I Corinthians/I Corinthians 1\|Ch. 1–2]] — "God's foolishness is wiser than human wisdom"; Christ crucified
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/46. I Corinthians/I Corinthians 11\|Ch. 11]] — The Lord's Supper
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/46. I Corinthians/I Corinthians 12\|Ch. 12]] — One body, many gifts; the Spirit distributes as he wills
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/46. I Corinthians/I Corinthians 13\|Ch. 13]] — "Love is patient, love is kind…" — the greatest gift
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/46. I Corinthians/I Corinthians 15\|Ch. 15]] — The resurrection: "Death is swallowed up in victory"

Cross-references: [[2. The Bible (ESV)/23. Isaiah/Isaiah 25\|Isaiah 25:8]] (death swallowed up), [[2. The Bible (ESV)/28. Hosea/Hosea 13\|Hosea 13:14]] (where is your sting?)

## 2 Corinthians (13 chapters)
**Date:** ~56 AD | **Themes:** [[6. Themes/Suffering & Perseverance]], [[6. Themes/Grace & Mercy]], [[6. Themes/Redemption & Salvation]]

The most personal of Paul's letters; defends his apostleship and describes the paradox of strength in weakness.
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/47. II Corinthians/II Corinthians 4\|Ch. 4]] — "We have this treasure in jars of clay"; "light and momentary troubles"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/47. II Corinthians/II Corinthians 5\|Ch. 5]] — New creation; the ministry of reconciliation; God made him sin for us
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/47. II Corinthians/II Corinthians 12\|Ch. 12]] — "My grace is sufficient… power is made perfect in weakness"

## Galatians (6 chapters)
**Date:** ~48–55 AD | **Themes:** [[6. Themes/Faith & Trust]], [[6. Themes/Grace & Mercy]], [[6. Themes/Law & Torah]], [[6. Themes/Covenant]], [[6. Themes/The Holy Spirit]]

"The Magna Carta of Christian liberty." Paul defends justification by faith alone against those requiring circumcision.
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/48. Galatians/Galatians 2\|Ch. 2]] — "Justified by faith in Christ, not by works of the law"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/48. Galatians/Galatians 3\|Ch. 3]] — Abraham believed; the law was a guardian until Christ; all one in Christ
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/48. Galatians/Galatians 5\|Ch. 5]] — The fruit of the Spirit; freedom vs. license

## Philippians (4 chapters)
**Date:** ~61–62 AD | **Themes:** [[6. Themes/Faith & Trust]], [[6. Themes/Suffering & Perseverance]], [[6. Themes/Prayer & Worship]]

The "Joy Letter" — written from prison, yet saturated with joy. The Christ hymn (ch. 2) is one of the NT's greatest passages.
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/50. Philippians/Philippians 2\|Ch. 2]] — The mind of Christ; "He humbled himself… God exalted him"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/50. Philippians/Philippians 4\|Ch. 4]] — "I can do all things through Christ"; "the peace of God which surpasses understanding"

- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/51. Colossians/Colossians 2\|Ch. 2]] — In Christ "all treasures of wisdom and knowledge are hidden"

## Philemon (1 chapter)
**Date:** ~60–62 AD | **Theme:** [[6. Themes/Grace & Mercy]], [[6. Themes/Love]]

A personal letter requesting that Philemon receive back his runaway slave Onesimus as "a dear brother." A miniature picture of the gospel: Paul intercedes, offering to pay any debt.
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/57. Philemon/Philemon 1\|Ch. 1]] — Paul's intercession for Onesimus
