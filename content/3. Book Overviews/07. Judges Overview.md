# Judges — Overview

**Author:** Unknown (possibly Samuel)
**Date:** Composed ~10th–7th century BC; events ~12th–11th century BC
**Genre:** Historical Narrative
**Key Themes:** [[Sin & The Fall]], [[Covenant]], [[Judgment]], [[Grace & Mercy]], [[Shepherd & King]]

## Summary
Judges tells the cyclical story of Israel after Joshua: the people abandon God, fall under oppression, cry out, are rescued by a judge (deliverer), and then return to sin. The refrain is "Every man did what was right in his own eyes." The book implicitly argues for the need for a godly king.

## The Cycle of Judges
**Sin → Oppression → Cry → Deliverance → Rest → Sin again**
