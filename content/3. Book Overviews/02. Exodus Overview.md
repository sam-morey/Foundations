# Exodus — Overview

**Author:** Moses (traditional)
**Date:** ~15th–13th century BC
**Genre:** Narrative / Law
**Key Themes:** [[Redemption & Salvation]], [[Covenant]], [[Law & Torah]], [[Sacrifice & Atonement]], [[The Holy Spirit]]

## Summary
Exodus narrates Israel's deliverance from slavery in Egypt — the paradigmatic OT redemption event — followed by the giving of the Law at Sinai and the construction of the Tabernacle, the place of God's dwelling among his people.

## Structure
| Section | Chapters | Content |
|---------|----------|---------|
| Oppression & the Call of Moses | 1–6 | Israel enslaved; the burning bush; Moses sent |
| The Plagues & the Passover | 7–13 | Ten plagues; death of firstborn; Passover |
| The Exodus & Wilderness | 14–18 | Crossing the sea; manna; water from the rock |
| The Sinai Covenant | 19–24 | The Law given; the Ten Commandments |
| Tabernacle Instructions | 25–31 | Detailed plans for the Tabernacle and priesthood |
| The Golden Calf & Renewal | 32–34 | Israel's idolatry; Moses intercedes; covenant renewed |
| Tabernacle Built | 35–40 | Construction completed; God's glory fills the Tabernacle |
