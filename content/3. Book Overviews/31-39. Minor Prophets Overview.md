# The Minor Prophets — Overview

The twelve "minor" prophets (minor in length, not importance) span four centuries of Israel's history, from the 9th century to the 5th. Together they address Israel's covenant failures, call for repentance, warn of judgment, and point forward to restoration and the coming Day of the Lord.

---

## [[28. Hosea Overview|Hosea]] (14 chapters)
**Date:** ~750–720 BC (northern kingdom) | **Theme:** [[Love]], [[Covenant]], [[Judgment]]
God commands Hosea to marry an unfaithful wife as a living parable of Israel's spiritual adultery. Despite everything, God's love pursues his people.
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/28 Hosea/Chapter_01\|Ch. 1]] — God's command to Hosea
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/28 Hosea/Chapter_11\|Ch. 11]] — "When Israel was a child, I loved him"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/28 Hosea/Chapter_14\|Ch. 14]] — A call to return; God's promise to heal

**NT:** [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40 Matthew/Chapter_02\|Matt 2:15]] ("Out of Egypt I called my son"), [[2. The Bible (ESV)/45 Romans/Chapter_09\|Romans 9:25]]

---

## [[29. Joel Overview|Joel]] (3 chapters)
**Date:** ~835 or ~400 BC (debated) | **Theme:** [[The Holy Spirit]], [[Judgment]], [[Prophecy & Fulfillment]]
A locust plague prompts a call to repentance. God promises to restore and pour out his Spirit on all flesh — fulfilled at Pentecost.
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/29 Joel/Chapter_02\|Ch. 2]] — "I will pour out my Spirit on all flesh" (quoted in Acts 2)
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/29 Joel/Chapter_03\|Ch. 3]] — Judgment of nations; restoration of Judah

---

## [[30. Amos Overview|Amos]] (9 chapters)
**Date:** ~760–750 BC | **Theme:** [[Justice & Righteousness]], [[Judgment]], [[Israel & The Gentiles]]
A shepherd-farmer called to prophesy against Israel's social injustice and hollow religion. "Let justice roll down like waters."
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/30 Amos/Chapter_05\|Ch. 5]] — "Seek good and not evil"; the great justice call
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/30 Amos/Chapter_09\|Ch. 9]] — The fallen booth of David to be restored

**NT:** [[2. The Bible (ESV)/44 Acts/Chapter_15\|Acts 15:16–17]] (Amos 9 quoted at Jerusalem Council)

---

## Obadiah (1 chapter)
**Date:** ~586 BC | **Theme:** [[Judgment]]
The shortest OT book; pronounces judgment on Edom for gloating over Jerusalem's fall.
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/31 Obadiah/Chapter_01\|Ch. 1]] — Edom condemned; "The day of the Lord is near upon all nations"

---

## [[32. Jonah Overview|Jonah]] (4 chapters)
**Date:** ~8th or ~5th century BC | **Theme:** [[Grace & Mercy]], [[Israel & The Gentiles]], [[Prayer & Worship]]
A reluctant prophet flees God's call to Nineveh; is swallowed by a fish; repents; preaches; watches Nineveh repent — then resents God's mercy. The book is about the shocking scope of God's grace.
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/32 Jonah/Chapter_01\|Ch. 1]] — Jonah flees; the storm; "Throw me into the sea"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/32 Jonah/Chapter_02\|Ch. 2]] — Jonah's prayer from the fish
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/32 Jonah/Chapter_03\|Ch. 3]] — Nineveh repents
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/32 Jonah/Chapter_04\|Ch. 4]] — Jonah's anger; God's compassion

**NT:** [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40 Matthew/Chapter_12\|Matt 12:39–41]] (the sign of Jonah), [[2. The Bible (ESV)/42 Luke/Chapter_11\|Luke 11:29–32]]

---

## Micah (7 chapters)
**Date:** ~740–700 BC | **Theme:** [[Justice & Righteousness]], [[Messiah & Christ]], [[Covenant]]
A contemporary of Isaiah, Micah condemns injustice and false prophets, announces judgment, but also promises restoration and the Messiah's birth in Bethlehem.
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/33 Micah/Chapter_05\|Ch. 5]] — "From you, Bethlehem… shall come forth a ruler in Israel"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/33 Micah/Chapter_06\|Ch. 6]] — "What does the Lord require? Do justice, love kindness, walk humbly"

**NT:** [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40 Matthew/Chapter_02\|Matt 2:6]] (Bethlehem prophecy)

---

## Nahum (3 chapters)
**Date:** ~663–612 BC | **Theme:** [[Judgment]]
An oracle against Nineveh (which had repented under Jonah but returned to violence). God is slow to anger but will not leave the guilty unpunished.
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/34 Nahum/Chapter_01\|Ch. 1]] — "The Lord is slow to anger… but will by no means clear the guilty"

---

## Habakkuk (3 chapters)
**Date:** ~605 BC | **Theme:** [[Faith & Trust]], [[Judgment]], [[Suffering & Perseverance]]
Habakkuk wrestles with God over evil and injustice — one of Scripture's most honest prophetic dialogues. He ends in radical trust despite all circumstances.
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/35 Habakkuk/Chapter_02\|Ch. 2]] — "The righteous shall live by his faith" (quoted in Romans, Galatians, Hebrews)
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/35 Habakkuk/Chapter_03\|Ch. 3]] — "Yet I will rejoice in the Lord… though the fig tree does not blossom"

**NT:** [[2. The Bible (ESV)/45 Romans/Chapter_01\|Rom 1:17]], [[2. The Bible (ESV)/48 Galatians/Chapter_03\|Gal 3:11]], [[2. The Bible (ESV)/58 Hebrews/Chapter_10\|Heb 10:38]]

---

## Zephaniah (3 chapters)
**Date:** ~640–609 BC | **Theme:** [[Judgment]], [[Grace & Mercy]]
The Day of the Lord looms large — first as universal judgment, then as joy for a purified remnant.
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/36 Zephaniah/Chapter_01\|Ch. 1]] — "The great day of the Lord is near"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/36 Zephaniah/Chapter_03\|Ch. 3]] — "The Lord your God is in your midst… he will rejoice over you with gladness"

---

## Haggai (2 chapters)
**Date:** ~520 BC | **Theme:** [[Prayer & Worship]], [[Covenant]]
A post-exilic prophet calling the returned exiles to complete the Temple rebuilding. Priorities matter: "Give careful thought to your ways."
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/37 Haggai/Chapter_01\|Ch. 1]] — Rebuilding the Temple called for
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/37 Haggai/Chapter_02\|Ch. 2]] — The latter glory of this house will surpass the former

---

## Zechariah (14 chapters)
**Date:** ~520–480 BC | **Theme:** [[Messiah & Christ]], [[Prophecy & Fulfillment]], [[Kingdom of God]]
The most messianic of the minor prophets. Eight visions and oracles looking ahead to the coming King, including the entry on a donkey, the betrayal for 30 pieces of silver, and the one pierced.
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/38 Zechariah/Chapter_09\|Ch. 9]] — The king comes riding on a donkey
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/38 Zechariah/Chapter_11\|Ch. 11]] — 30 pieces of silver
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/38 Zechariah/Chapter_12\|Ch. 12]] — "They will look on me, the one they have pierced"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/38 Zechariah/Chapter_14\|Ch. 14]] — The Lord will be King over all the earth

**NT:** [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40 Matthew/Chapter_21\|Matt 21:5]], [[2. The Bible (ESV)/43 John/Chapter_19\|John 19:37]], [[2. The Bible (ESV)/66 Revelation of John/Chapter_01\|Rev 1:7]]

---

## Malachi (4 chapters)
**Date:** ~450–430 BC | **Theme:** [[Covenant]], [[Prophecy & Fulfillment]], [[Judgment]]
The last OT prophet. Israel has grown cold; the priests are corrupt. God promises a messenger to prepare the way and Elijah to come before the great Day. The NT begins four centuries later with John the Baptist fulfilling these words.
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/39 Malachi/Chapter_03\|Ch. 3]] — "Behold, I send my messenger to prepare the way"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/39 Malachi/Chapter_04\|Ch. 4]] — Elijah to come; "The sun of righteousness shall rise with healing in its wings"

**NT:** [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40 Matthew/Chapter_11\|Matt 11:10,14]] (John the Baptist is Elijah/the messenger), [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40 Matthew/Chapter_03\|Matt 3:1–3]]
