# 2 Samuel — Overview

**Author:** Unknown
**Date:** ~10th–9th century BC
**Genre:** Historical Narrative
**Key Themes:** [[Covenant]], [[Shepherd & King]], [[Sin & The Fall]], [[Grace & Mercy]], [[Messiah & Christ]]

## Summary
2 Samuel follows David's reign over all Israel — from his consolidation of power to the Davidic covenant, and then through his catastrophic sin with Bathsheba and its consequences. David's story is both a model of faithfulness and a warning about the corruption of power.
