# General Epistles — Overview

The General Epistles (James through Jude) are seven letters written to the broader church. They address practical faith, perseverance under trial, false teaching, the Christian hope, and holy living.

## Notable People
| Person | Role | Why They Matter |
|--------|------|-----------------|
| James | Author of James; brother of Jesus | Once a skeptic during Jesus' ministry (John 7:5), his resurrection appearance transformed him; he became the pillar of the Jerusalem church and wrote the NT's most practical wisdom letter |
| [[4. People/Peter\|Peter]] | Author of 1 & 2 Peter | The chief apostle writes to scattered believers facing persecution, grounding their endurance in Christ's own suffering; 2 Peter is likely his final letter before martyrdom |
| [[4. People/John the Apostle\|John]] | Author of 1, 2, & 3 John | Writing in old age, John distills a lifetime of witness to Jesus into three tests of genuine faith: right belief, obedience, and love — "God is love" |
| Jude | Author of Jude; brother of Jesus | Like James, a brother of Jesus who came to faith after the resurrection; his single urgent chapter calls believers to contend for the faith against those who have crept in unnoticed |

## James (5 chapters)
**Author:** James, brother of Jesus | **Date:** ~45–50 AD
**Themes:** [[Faith & Trust]], [[Justice & Righteousness]], [[Wisdom]], [[Suffering & Perseverance]]

The most practical NT letter — wisdom in the tradition of Proverbs, focused on the lived implications of faith.
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/59. James/James 1\|Ch. 1]] — "Count it all joy when you face trials"; ask God for wisdom; be doers, not hearers only
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/59. James/James 2\|Ch. 2]] — "Faith without works is dead"; no partiality; love your neighbor
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/59. James/James 3\|Ch. 3]] — Taming the tongue; wisdom from above
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/59. James/James 5\|Ch. 5]] — The prayer of faith; "Elijah was a man just like us"

## 1 Peter (5 chapters)
**Author:** Peter the Apostle | **Date:** ~60–65 AD
**Themes:** [[Suffering & Perseverance]], [[Redemption & Salvation]], [[Covenant]], [[Sacrifice & Atonement]]

Written to Christians facing persecution throughout Asia Minor. Encourages holy living as sojourners and aliens, grounded in Christ's suffering and resurrection.
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/60. I Peter/I Peter 1\|Ch. 1]] — Ransomed by the precious blood of Christ; a living hope through resurrection
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/60. I Peter/I Peter 2\|Ch. 2]] — Living stones; "A royal priesthood, a holy nation"; Christ as cornerstone
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/60. I Peter/I Peter 4\|Ch. 4]] — Rejoice to share in Christ's sufferings
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/60. I Peter/I Peter 5\|Ch. 5]] — "Cast all your anxiety on him… The devil prowls like a roaring lion"

Cross-references: 1 Peter 2 ← [[2. The Bible (ESV)/02. Exodus/Exodus 19\|Exodus 19]] (kingdom of priests), [[2. The Bible (ESV)/23. Isaiah/Isaiah 28\|Isaiah 28:16]] (cornerstone)

## 2 Peter (3 chapters)
**Author:** Peter | **Date:** ~65–68 AD
**Themes:** [[The Word of God]], [[Judgment]], [[Suffering & Perseverance]]

A warning against false teachers; insists Scripture came from God; the Day of the Lord will come like a thief.
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/61. II Peter/II Peter 1\|Ch. 1]] — Prophecy "carried along by the Holy Spirit"; grow in grace and knowledge
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/61. II Peter/II Peter 3\|Ch. 3]] — "A thousand years is as a day"; the Lord is patient; new heavens and new earth

## 1 John (5 chapters)
**Author:** John the Apostle | **Date:** ~90–95 AD
**Themes:** [[Love]], [[Light & Darkness]], [[Faith & Trust]], [[The Holy Spirit]]

A letter of assurance and discernment. Three "tests" of genuine Christianity: belief, obedience, love. "God is love" is stated here.
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/62. I John/I John 1\|Ch. 1]] — "God is light"; walking in the light; confession and cleansing
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/62. I John/I John 3\|Ch. 3]] — "We shall be like him… everyone who thus hopes purifies himself"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/62. I John/I John 4\|Ch. 4]] — "God is love"; test the spirits; we love because he first loved us
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/62. I John/I John 5\|Ch. 5]] — "Whoever has the Son has life"; this is the victory — our faith
