# General Epistles — Overview

The General Epistles (James through Jude) are seven letters written to the broader church. They address practical faith, perseverance under trial, false teaching, the Christian hope, and holy living.

---

## James (5 chapters)
**Author:** James, brother of Jesus | **Date:** ~45–50 AD
**Themes:** [[Faith & Trust]], [[Justice & Righteousness]], [[Wisdom]], [[Suffering & Perseverance]]

The most practical NT letter — wisdom in the tradition of Proverbs, focused on the lived implications of faith.
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/59 James/Chapter_01\|Ch. 1]] — "Count it all joy when you face trials"; ask God for wisdom; be doers, not hearers only
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/59 James/Chapter_02\|Ch. 2]] — "Faith without works is dead"; no partiality; love your neighbor
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/59 James/Chapter_03\|Ch. 3]] — Taming the tongue; wisdom from above
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/59 James/Chapter_05\|Ch. 5]] — The prayer of faith; "Elijah was a man just like us"

## 1 Peter (5 chapters)
**Author:** Peter the Apostle | **Date:** ~60–65 AD
**Themes:** [[Suffering & Perseverance]], [[Redemption & Salvation]], [[Covenant]], [[Sacrifice & Atonement]]

Written to Christians facing persecution throughout Asia Minor. Encourages holy living as sojourners and aliens, grounded in Christ's suffering and resurrection.
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/60 I Peter/Chapter_01\|Ch. 1]] — Ransomed by the precious blood of Christ; a living hope through resurrection
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/60 I Peter/Chapter_02\|Ch. 2]] — Living stones; "A royal priesthood, a holy nation"; Christ as cornerstone
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/60 I Peter/Chapter_04\|Ch. 4]] — Rejoice to share in Christ's sufferings
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/60 I Peter/Chapter_05\|Ch. 5]] — "Cast all your anxiety on him… The devil prowls like a roaring lion"

Cross-references: 1 Peter 2 ← [[2. The Bible (ESV)/02 Exodus/Chapter_19\|Exodus 19]] (kingdom of priests), [[2. The Bible (ESV)/23 Isaiah/Chapter_28\|Isaiah 28:16]] (cornerstone)

## 2 Peter (3 chapters)
**Author:** Peter | **Date:** ~65–68 AD
**Themes:** [[The Word of God]], [[Judgment]], [[Suffering & Perseverance]]

A warning against false teachers; insists Scripture came from God; the Day of the Lord will come like a thief.
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/61 II Peter/Chapter_01\|Ch. 1]] — Prophecy "carried along by the Holy Spirit"; grow in grace and knowledge
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/61 II Peter/Chapter_03\|Ch. 3]] — "A thousand years is as a day"; the Lord is patient; new heavens and new earth

## 1 John (5 chapters)
**Author:** John the Apostle | **Date:** ~90–95 AD
**Themes:** [[Love]], [[Light & Darkness]], [[Faith & Trust]], [[The Holy Spirit]]

A letter of assurance and discernment. Three "tests" of genuine Christianity: belief, obedience, love. "God is love" is stated here.
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/62 I John/Chapter_01\|Ch. 1]] — "God is light"; walking in the light; confession and cleansing
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/62 I John/Chapter_03\|Ch. 3]] — "We shall be like him… everyone who thus hopes purifies himself"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/62 I John/Chapter_04\|Ch. 4]] — "God is love"; test the spirits; we love because he first loved us
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/62 I John/Chapter_05\|Ch. 5]] — "Whoever has the Son has life"; this is the victory — our faith
