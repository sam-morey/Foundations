# John — Overview

**Author:** John the Apostle (the "Beloved Disciple")
**Date:** ~85–95 AD
**Genre:** Gospel
**Key Themes:** [[Messiah & Christ]], [[Faith & Trust]], [[Love]], [[Resurrection & Eternal Life]], [[The Holy Spirit]], [[Light & Darkness]]

## Summary
John's Gospel is the most theological of the four. It opens with the cosmic prologue ("In the beginning was the Word") and structures itself around seven signs and seven "I AM" sayings. Its purpose is explicit: "These are written that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and that by believing you may have life in his name" (20:31).

## Structure
| Section | Chapters | Content |
|---------|----------|---------|
| Prologue | 1:1–18 | The eternal Word; "The Word became flesh" |
| The Book of Signs | 1–12 | Seven signs; public ministry; growing opposition |
| The Farewell Discourse | 13–17 | Final teaching; the Holy Spirit promised; Jesus' prayer |
| The Book of Glory | 18–20 | Passion, resurrection |
| Epilogue | 21 | Restoration of Peter |
