# Isaiah — Overview

**Author:** Isaiah son of Amoz (chs. 1–39 primarily); chs. 40–66 addressed to exilic/post-exilic audience
**Date:** ~740–700 BC (ministry); later sections may be 6th century
**Genre:** Prophecy / Poetry
**Key Themes:** [[Messiah & Christ]], [[Redemption & Salvation]], [[Covenant]], [[Prophecy & Fulfillment]], [[Creation & New Creation]], [[Israel & The Gentiles]], [[Justice & Righteousness]]

## Summary
Isaiah is the NT's most-quoted OT book. It moves from judgment on Judah and the nations (1–39) to magnificent consolation and hope (40–66). The Servant Songs (42, 49, 50, 53) describe a figure who suffers for others — ultimately fulfilled in Jesus Christ. The book ends with visions of new creation and universal worship.

## Structure
| Section | Chapters | Focus |
|---------|----------|-------|
| Book of Judgment | 1–39 | Judgment on Judah, surrounding nations, and the world |
| Book of Comfort | 40–55 | Salvation, Servant Songs, New Exodus, New Covenant |
| Book of Consummation | 56–66 | New creation, universal worship, eternal glory |
