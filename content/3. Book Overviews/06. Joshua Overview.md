# Joshua — Overview

**Author:** Joshua / later editors
**Date:** ~13th–11th century BC
**Genre:** Historical Narrative
**Key Themes:** [[The Promised Land & Rest]], [[Faith & Trust]], [[Covenant]], [[Judgment]]

## Summary
Joshua records Israel's conquest of Canaan under Joshua's leadership after forty years in the wilderness. It is a book of covenant faithfulness — God delivers on his promises — and of covenant judgment on the Canaanite nations. It ends with Joshua's farewell challenge: "Choose this day whom you will serve."
