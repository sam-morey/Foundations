# Ezekiel — Overview

**Author:** Ezekiel son of Buzi, a priest
**Date:** ~593–571 BC (in Babylon during exile)
**Genre:** Prophecy / Apocalyptic / Visionary
**Key Themes:** [[Judgment]], [[Covenant]], [[The Holy Spirit]], [[Prophecy & Fulfillment]], [[Creation & New Creation]]

## Summary
Ezekiel ministers to the exiles in Babylon. His book opens with an overwhelming vision of God's glory (the chariot-throne). He pronounces judgment on Jerusalem and the nations, witnesses God's glory departing the Temple — and then promises its dramatic return. The New Covenant promises (36–37) and the vision of the new Temple (40–48) anticipate complete renewal.
