# Numbers — Overview

**Author:** Moses (traditional)
**Date:** ~15th–13th century BC
**Genre:** Narrative / Law
**Key Themes:** [[Faith & Trust]], [[Judgment]], [[The Promised Land & Rest]], [[Covenant]]

## Summary
Numbers records Israel's 40-year wilderness journey between Sinai and the plains of Moab. The central crisis is the failure to trust God and enter the land (ch. 13–14), which results in a generation dying in the wilderness. It is a story of grace and judgment, doubt and faithfulness.
