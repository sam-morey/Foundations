# Revelation — Overview

**Author:** John the Apostle (exiled on Patmos)
**Date:** ~95 AD
**Genre:** Apocalyptic / Prophecy / Epistle
**Key Themes:** [[Kingdom of God]], [[Messiah & Christ]], [[Judgment]], [[Resurrection & Eternal Life]], [[Suffering & Perseverance]], [[Creation & New Creation]], [[Prophecy & Fulfillment]], [[Prayer & Worship]]

## Summary
Revelation is the culmination of the entire biblical story. Written to churches facing Roman persecution, it unveils the heavenly reality behind earthly events — Christ is Lord, not Caesar. Using symbolic imagery drawn from Daniel, Ezekiel, Isaiah, and Zechariah, it depicts the struggle between the Lamb and the Beast, and ends with the new creation — "Behold, I am making all things new."
