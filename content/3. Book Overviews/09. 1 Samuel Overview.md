# 1 Samuel — Overview

**Author:** Unknown (possibly Samuel, Gad, Nathan)
**Date:** ~10th–9th century BC
**Genre:** Historical Narrative
**Key Themes:** [[Shepherd & King]], [[Covenant]], [[Faith & Trust]], [[Judgment]]

## Summary
1 Samuel records the transition from the period of judges to the monarchy. It follows Samuel the prophet-judge, Saul the failed king, and the rise of David — the shepherd-king after God's own heart.
