# Sin & The Fall

Sin is humanity's rebellion against God — a fundamental rupture in the relationship between Creator and creature. The Fall in Genesis 3 explains universal human brokenness, and the rest of Scripture traces God's response to it.

## Key Passages

### The Fall
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/01 Genesis/Chapter_03\|Genesis 3]] — The serpent, the forbidden fruit, the first sin, and the curse
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/01 Genesis/Chapter_04\|Genesis 4]] — Cain murders Abel; sin spreads immediately

### Sin's Universal Scope
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/19 Psalms/Chapter_14\|Psalm 14]] — "There is none who does good, not even one"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/19 Psalms/Chapter_51\|Psalm 51]] — David's confession after adultery and murder
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/23 Isaiah/Chapter_53\|Isaiah 53]] — "All we like sheep have gone astray"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/45 Romans/Chapter_01\|Romans 1]] — Universal suppression of truth and moral decline
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/45 Romans/Chapter_03\|Romans 3]] — All have sinned and fall short of God's glory
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/45 Romans/Chapter_05\|Romans 5]] — Sin and death entered through one man (Adam)
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/45 Romans/Chapter_07\|Romans 7]] — The inner struggle with sin

### The Consequences of Sin
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/01 Genesis/Chapter_03\|Genesis 3]] — Spiritual death, broken relationships, physical labor and pain
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/23 Isaiah/Chapter_59\|Isaiah 59]] — Iniquities separate from God
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/45 Romans/Chapter_06\|Romans 6]] — The wages of sin is death
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/49 Ephesians/Chapter_02\|Ephesians 2]] — Humanity dead in trespasses and sins

### God's Response to Sin
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/01 Genesis/Chapter_03\|Genesis 3:15]] — First promise of a Redeemer (*protevangelium*)
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/03 Leviticus/Chapter_16\|Leviticus 16]] — Day of Atonement; covering sin
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/23 Isaiah/Chapter_53\|Isaiah 53]] — The Servant bears the sin of many
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/43 John/Chapter_01\|John 1]] — Behold the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/62 I John/Chapter_01\|1 John 1]] — Confession and cleansing from sin
