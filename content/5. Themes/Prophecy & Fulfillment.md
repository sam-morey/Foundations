# Prophecy & Fulfillment

Biblical prophecy is not primarily fortune-telling but God speaking through human messengers to call his people to faithfulness and to declare what he will do. The pattern of promise-and-fulfillment runs through the entire Bible.

## Key Passages

### Major Prophetic Books
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/23. Isaiah/Isaiah 1\|Isaiah]] — The great Evangelical Prophet; Messiah, Servant, New Creation
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/24. Jeremiah/Jeremiah 1\|Jeremiah]] — Coming judgment and the New Covenant
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/26. Ezekiel/Ezekiel 1\|Ezekiel]] — Visions of God's glory departing and returning; new heart; dry bones
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/27. Daniel/Daniel 1\|Daniel]] — The God who rules history; visions of world empires and the Son of Man

- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40. Matthew/Matthew 1\|Matthew 1]] — Born of a virgin (Isaiah 7)

- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/42. Luke/Luke 24\|Luke 24]] — Jesus explains from Moses and the Prophets things concerning himself

### The Fulfillment Pattern
Matthew's gospel in particular uses an **"fulfillment formula"**: "This was to fulfill what was spoken by the prophet…"
See: [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40. Matthew/Matthew 1\|Matt 1:22]], [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40. Matthew/Matthew 2\|Matt 2:15]], [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40. Matthew/Matthew 2\|Matt 2:17]], [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40. Matthew/Matthew 4\|Matt 4:14]], [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40. Matthew/Matthew 12\|Matt 12:17]]
