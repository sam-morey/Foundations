# Sacrifice & Atonement

Atonement means "at-one-ment" — restoring a broken relationship. In Scripture, this is accomplished through sacrifice: a substitute that absorbs the consequences of sin so that the guilty may stand before a holy God.

## Key Passages

### Sacrificial System Established
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/01 Genesis/Chapter_04\|Genesis 4]] — Cain and Abel's offerings; sacrifice from the beginning
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/01 Genesis/Chapter_22\|Genesis 22]] — The Akedah (binding of Isaac); God provides a substitute ram
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/02 Exodus/Chapter_12\|Exodus 12]] — The Passover lamb; blood on the doorposts = protection from judgment
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/03 Leviticus/Chapter_01\|Leviticus 1–7]] — The five main offerings: burnt, grain, peace, sin, guilt
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/03 Leviticus/Chapter_16\|Leviticus 16]] — The Day of Atonement (*Yom Kippur*): the high priest enters the Holy of Holies; the scapegoat
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/03 Leviticus/Chapter_17\|Leviticus 17]] — "The life of the flesh is in the blood… it is the blood that makes atonement"

### Prophetic Transformation
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/23 Isaiah/Chapter_53\|Isaiah 53]] — The Servant is pierced for our transgressions; the ultimate sacrificial substitute
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/19 Psalms/Chapter_51\|Psalm 51]] — The sacrifice God truly desires: a broken and contrite heart
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/19 Psalms/Chapter_40\|Psalm 40]] — "Sacrifice and offering you did not desire… I have come" (cited in Hebrews 10)

### Christ as the Fulfillment
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/43 John/Chapter_01\|John 1]] — "Behold, the Lamb of God, who takes away the sin of the world"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40 Matthew/Chapter_26\|Matthew 26]] — The Last Supper: "This is my blood of the covenant, poured out for many for the forgiveness of sins"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/45 Romans/Chapter_03\|Romans 3]] — God put Christ forward as a *propitiation* (mercy seat)
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/45 Romans/Chapter_05\|Romans 5]] — Christ died for the ungodly
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/47 II Corinthians/Chapter_05\|2 Corinthians 5]] — God made him who knew no sin to be sin for us
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/58 Hebrews/Chapter_09\|Hebrews 9]] — Christ entered the heavenly Holy of Holies with his own blood; once for all
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/58 Hebrews/Chapter_10\|Hebrews 10]] — The old sacrifices could not take away sin; Christ's one sacrifice perfects forever
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/60 I Peter/Chapter_01\|1 Peter 1]] — "The precious blood of Christ, like that of a lamb without blemish"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/66 Revelation of John/Chapter_05\|Revelation 5]] — The Lamb slain yet standing; worthy to open the scroll

## The Logic of Substitution
The OT system was typological — the animals could not truly remove sin (Hebrews 10:4) but they pointed forward to the one sacrifice that could. Christ is:
- The **Passover Lamb** ([[2. The Bible (ESV)/02 Exodus/Chapter_12\|Exodus 12]]; [[2. The Bible (ESV)/46 I Corinthians/Chapter_05\|1 Corinthians 5:7]])
- The **Day of Atonement scapegoat** ([[2. The Bible (ESV)/03 Leviticus/Chapter_16\|Leviticus 16]]; [[2. The Bible (ESV)/58 Hebrews/Chapter_09\|Hebrews 9]])
- The **Suffering Servant** ([[2. The Bible (ESV)/23 Isaiah/Chapter_53\|Isaiah 53]])
- The **High Priest and the Sacrifice** simultaneously ([[2. The Bible (ESV)/58 Hebrews/Chapter_07\|Hebrews 7]])
