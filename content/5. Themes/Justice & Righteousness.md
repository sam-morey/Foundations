# Justice & Righteousness

The Hebrew words *mishpat* (justice) and *tzedakah* (righteousness) appear together hundreds of times in the OT. True religion is never merely ritual — it produces justice for the vulnerable and righteous ordering of society. God is both the standard of righteousness and the champion of justice.

## Key Passages

### God as Just and Righteous
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/01 Genesis/Chapter_18\|Genesis 18]] — "Shall not the Judge of all the earth do what is just?"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/05 Deuteronomy/Chapter_10\|Deuteronomy 10]] — God executes justice for the fatherless and widow; loves the sojourner
- [[Chapter_89\|Psalm 89]] — "Righteousness and justice are the foundation of your throne"
- [[Chapter_97\|Psalm 97]] — "Righteousness and justice are the foundation of his throne"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/23 Isaiah/Chapter_30\|Isaiah 30]] — "The Lord is a God of justice"

### The Call for Social Justice
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/02 Exodus/Chapter_22\|Exodus 22]] — Protect the widow, orphan, and sojourner; lend without interest
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/03 Leviticus/Chapter_19\|Leviticus 19]] — Leave grain for the poor; use honest weights; love your neighbor
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/23 Isaiah/Chapter_01\|Isaiah 1]] — God refuses hollow worship while justice is neglected; "Seek justice, correct oppression"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/23 Isaiah/Chapter_58\|Isaiah 58]] — True fasting: loosing bonds of injustice, feeding the hungry
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/24 Jeremiah/Chapter_22\|Jeremiah 22]] — Doing justice and righteousness is knowing God
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/30 Amos/Chapter_05\|Amos 5]] — "Let justice roll down like waters, and righteousness like an ever-flowing stream"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/33 Micah/Chapter_06\|Micah 6]] — "What does the Lord require of you but to do justice, to love kindness, and to walk humbly with your God?"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/38 Zechariah/Chapter_07\|Zechariah 7]] — Show kindness and mercy; do not oppress the vulnerable

### Jesus and Justice
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40 Matthew/Chapter_05\|Matthew 5]] — "Blessed are those who hunger and thirst for righteousness"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40 Matthew/Chapter_23\|Matthew 23]] — Jesus condemns neglecting "the weightier matters of the law: justice, mercy, and faithfulness"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40 Matthew/Chapter_25\|Matthew 25]] — The sheep and the goats: "as you did it to one of the least of these, you did it to me"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/42 Luke/Chapter_04\|Luke 4]] — Jesus announces good news to the poor, release to the captives

### Righteousness in Paul
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/45 Romans/Chapter_01\|Romans 1]] — The righteousness of God is revealed in the gospel
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/45 Romans/Chapter_03\|Romans 3]] — God is just and the justifier of the one who has faith
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/47 II Corinthians/Chapter_05\|2 Corinthians 5]] — "We might become the righteousness of God"

### James and the Practical Dimension
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/59 James/Chapter_01\|James 1]] — Pure religion: care for orphans and widows
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/59 James/Chapter_02\|James 2]] — Don't show partiality to the rich over the poor
