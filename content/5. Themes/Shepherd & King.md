# Shepherd & King

The shepherd is one of Scripture's central images for leadership — a figure who protects, guides, and lays down their life for the flock. Israel's kings were to be shepherd-kings (after the model of David), but they consistently failed. God himself steps in as the true Shepherd, fulfilled in Jesus Christ.

## Key Passages

### God as Shepherd
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/01. Genesis/Genesis 48\|Genesis 48]] — "God who has been my shepherd all my life long"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/19. Psalms/Psalms 23\|Psalm 23]] — "The Lord is my shepherd; I shall not want" — the paradigmatic shepherd psalm
- [[Psalms 80\|Psalm 80]] — "Give ear, O Shepherd of Israel, you who lead Joseph like a flock"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/23. Isaiah/Isaiah 40\|Isaiah 40]] — "He will tend his flock like a shepherd; he will gather the lambs"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/26. Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34\|Ezekiel 34]] — God condemns the false shepherds of Israel and promises to shepherd his people himself; the coming Davidic shepherd

### David: The Shepherd-King
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/09. I Samuel/I Samuel 16\|1 Samuel 16]] — David chosen from the sheep; the shepherd becomes king
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/10. II Samuel/II Samuel 5\|2 Samuel 5]] — David becomes king over all Israel
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/10. II Samuel/II Samuel 7\|2 Samuel 7]] — The Davidic covenant: an eternal dynasty

### Failed Shepherds
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/24. Jeremiah/Jeremiah 23\|Jeremiah 23]] — Woe to the shepherds who destroy and scatter God's flock; God will raise a righteous Branch
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/26. Ezekiel/Ezekiel 34\|Ezekiel 34]] — The full indictment of Israel's shepherds; God takes the flock back himself
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/38. Zechariah/Zechariah 11\|Zechariah 11]] — The foolish shepherd; the good shepherd rejected for thirty pieces of silver

### Jesus as the Good Shepherd
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40. Matthew/Matthew 9\|Matthew 9]] — Jesus saw the crowds "like sheep without a shepherd" and had compassion
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40. Matthew/Matthew 25\|Matthew 25]] — The shepherd separates sheep from goats
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40. Matthew/Matthew 26\|Matthew 26]] — "Strike the shepherd and the sheep will be scattered" (Zechariah 13)
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/43. John/John 10\|John 10]] — "I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep"; one flock, one shepherd
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/42. Luke/Luke 15\|Luke 15]] — The parable of the lost sheep; the shepherd who leaves ninety-nine to find one

### The Shepherd in the Letters and Revelation
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/58. Hebrews/Hebrews 13\|Hebrews 13]] — "The great Shepherd of the sheep" raised from the dead
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/60. I Peter/I Peter 2\|1 Peter 2]] — "You were straying like sheep, but have now returned to the Shepherd and Overseer of your souls"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/60. I Peter/I Peter 5\|1 Peter 5]] — Elders are to shepherd the flock; "when the chief Shepherd appears"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/66. Revelation of John/Revelation of John 7\|Revelation 7]] — "The Lamb in the midst of the throne will be their shepherd"
