# Light & Darkness

Light and darkness are among Scripture's most pervasive images. God is light; darkness represents sin, ignorance, evil, and death. The coming of Christ is the arrival of true light into a dark world.

## Key Passages

### Light in Creation
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/01 Genesis/Chapter_01\|Genesis 1]] — "Let there be light" — God's first creative act; light is separated from darkness
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/19 Psalms/Chapter_36\|Psalm 36]] — "In your light do we see light"
- [[Chapter_104\|Psalm 104]] — God covers himself with light as a garment

### God as Light
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/19 Psalms/Chapter_27\|Psalm 27]] — "The Lord is my light and my salvation"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/23 Isaiah/Chapter_60\|Isaiah 60]] — "The Lord will be your everlasting light"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/62 I John/Chapter_01\|1 John 1]] — "God is light, and in him is no darkness at all"

### Darkness as Sin and Judgment
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/02 Exodus/Chapter_10\|Exodus 10]] — Darkness as the ninth plague over Egypt
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/23 Isaiah/Chapter_05\|Isaiah 5]] — "Woe to those who call evil good and good evil, who put darkness for light"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/23 Isaiah/Chapter_59\|Isaiah 59]] — "We grope like those who have no eyes… we are in dark places like those who are dead"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/30 Amos/Chapter_05\|Amos 5]] — "The day of the Lord is darkness, not light"

### The Coming Light (Messianic)
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/23 Isaiah/Chapter_09\|Isaiah 9]] — "The people who walked in darkness have seen a great light"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/23 Isaiah/Chapter_42\|Isaiah 42]] — The Servant is "a light for the nations"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/23 Isaiah/Chapter_60\|Isaiah 60]] — "Arise, shine, for your light has come"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/33 Micah/Chapter_07\|Micah 7]] — "When I sit in darkness, the Lord will be a light to me"

### Jesus as the Light of the World
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40 Matthew/Chapter_04\|Matthew 4]] — Jesus begins ministry in Galilee; fulfillment of Isaiah 9
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40 Matthew/Chapter_05\|Matthew 5]] — "You are the light of the world"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/43 John/Chapter_01\|John 1]] — "The true light, which gives light to everyone, was coming into the world"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/43 John/Chapter_08\|John 8]] — "I am the light of the world"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/43 John/Chapter_03\|John 3]] — People love darkness rather than light because their deeds are evil
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/43 John/Chapter_09\|John 9]] — Jesus heals a blind man: a sign of bringing light

### The Life of Light
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/45 Romans/Chapter_13\|Romans 13]] — "Let us cast off the works of darkness and put on the armor of light"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/49 Ephesians/Chapter_05\|Ephesians 5]] — "Walk as children of light"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/62 I John/Chapter_01\|1 John 1]] — Walking in the light as God is in the light; fellowship and cleansing

### Light in the New Creation
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/66 Revelation of John/Chapter_21\|Revelation 21–22]] — The new Jerusalem has no need of sun or moon; the glory of God is its light; the Lamb is its lamp
