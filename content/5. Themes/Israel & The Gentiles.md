# Israel & The Gentiles

God chose Israel not as an end in itself but as the means of blessing all nations. The tension between particular election (Israel) and universal scope (all peoples) runs through the entire Bible and finds its resolution in Christ and the Church.

## Key Passages

### The Universal Purpose of Israel's Election
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/01. Genesis/Genesis 12\|Genesis 12]] — "In you all the families of the earth shall be blessed"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/02. Exodus/Exodus 19\|Exodus 19]] — Israel is a "kingdom of priests" — a mediating nation
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/23. Isaiah/Isaiah 49\|Isaiah 49]] — The Servant is "a light for the nations"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/23. Isaiah/Isaiah 56\|Isaiah 56]] — Foreigners who join themselves to the Lord will be accepted

### Gentiles in the Old Testament
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/01. Genesis/Genesis 14\|Genesis 14]] — Melchizedek, priest of God Most High, blesses Abraham
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/08. Ruth/Ruth 1\|Ruth 1]] — Ruth the Moabite joins Israel ("Your people shall be my people")
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/32. Jonah/Jonah 3\|Jonah 3–4]] — God's mercy extended to Nineveh; Jonah's resistance
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/23. Isaiah/Isaiah 2\|Isaiah 2]] — "All nations shall flow to the mountain of the Lord"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/23. Isaiah/Isaiah 60\|Isaiah 60]] — The nations come to Israel's light
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/38. Zechariah/Zechariah 8\|Zechariah 8]] — Ten men from every nation take hold of a Jew's garment saying "God is with you"

### Jesus Breaks the Barrier
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40. Matthew/Matthew 8\|Matthew 8]] — Jesus heals the Roman centurion; praises his great faith
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40. Matthew/Matthew 15\|Matthew 15]] — The Canaanite woman's faith; Jesus heals her daughter
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/43. John/John 4\|John 4]] — Jesus speaks with the Samaritan woman; many Samaritans believe
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40. Matthew/Matthew 28\|Matthew 28]] — The Great Commission: "Go and make disciples of all nations"

### The Church as Jew and Gentile United
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/44. Acts/Acts 10\|Acts 10]] — Peter's vision and Cornelius: the Gentiles receive the Spirit
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/44. Acts/Acts 15\|Acts 15]] — The Jerusalem Council: Gentiles do not need to become Jews to be saved
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/45. Romans/Romans 9\|Romans 9–11]] — The mystery of Israel: hardening, the Gentiles' fullness, and all Israel saved
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/48. Galatians/Galatians 3\|Galatians 3]] — "There is neither Jew nor Greek… you are all one in Christ Jesus"; Abraham's true offspring
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/49. Ephesians/Ephesians 2\|Ephesians 2]] — The "dividing wall of hostility" broken down; one new humanity
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/49. Ephesians/Ephesians 3\|Ephesians 3]] — The mystery hidden for ages: Gentiles are fellow heirs with Israel
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/66. Revelation of John/Revelation of John 7\|Revelation 7]] — A great multitude from every nation, tribe, people, and language
