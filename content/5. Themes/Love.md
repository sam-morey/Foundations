# Love

The Bible declares that "God is love" (1 John 4:8). Love is not merely an attribute God possesses — it is his very nature. The call to love God and neighbor stands at the center of both Old and New Testament ethics.

## Key Passages

### God's Love in the Old Testament
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/05. Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 6\|Deuteronomy 6]] — "Love the Lord your God with all your heart, soul, and might" (the *Shema*)
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/05. Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 7\|Deuteronomy 7]] — God chose Israel not because of their greatness but because he loved them
- [[Psalms 103\|Psalm 103]] — "As a father shows compassion to his children, so the Lord shows compassion"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/28. Hosea/Hosea 11\|Hosea 11]] — God's anguished, tender love for wayward Israel
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/24. Jeremiah/Jeremiah 31\|Jeremiah 31]] — "I have loved you with an everlasting love"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/22. Song of Solomon/Song of Solomon 1\|Song of Solomon]] — The beauty of human love; an allegory of God's love for his people

### Love of Neighbor
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/03. Leviticus/Leviticus 19\|Leviticus 19]] — "You shall love your neighbor as yourself"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/05. Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 10\|Deuteronomy 10]] — "Love the sojourner, for you were sojourners in Egypt"

### Jesus on Love
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40. Matthew/Matthew 5\|Matthew 5]] — Love your enemies; pray for those who persecute you
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40. Matthew/Matthew 22\|Matthew 22]] — The two great commandments: love God, love neighbor
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/43. John/John 3\|John 3]] — "For God so loved the world that he gave his only Son"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/43. John/John 13\|John 13]] — "Love one another as I have loved you"; a new commandment
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/43. John/John 15\|John 15]] — "Greater love has no one than this: to lay down one's life for one's friends"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/42. Luke/Luke 10\|Luke 10]] — The Good Samaritan: defining who my neighbor is

### Paul on Love
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/45. Romans/Romans 5\|Romans 5]] — "God demonstrates his love for us in that while we were still sinners, Christ died for us"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/45. Romans/Romans 8\|Romans 8]] — Nothing can separate us from the love of God in Christ Jesus
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/46. I Corinthians/I Corinthians 13\|1 Corinthians 13]] — The great hymn to love: patient, kind, not self-seeking, endures all things
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/49. Ephesians/Ephesians 3\|Ephesians 3]] — That you may know the love of Christ that surpasses knowledge

### John on Love
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/62. I John/I John 3\|1 John 3]] — We love because he first loved us; love in deed, not only word
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/62. I John/I John 4\|1 John 4]] — "God is love"; "We love because he first loved us"

## The Two Great Commands
[[2. The Bible (ESV)/40. Matthew/Matthew 22\|Matthew 22:37–40]]:
1. **Love God** — with all your heart, soul, and mind (from [[2. The Bible (ESV)/05. Deuteronomy/Deuteronomy 6\|Deuteronomy 6]])
2. **Love your neighbor** — as yourself (from [[2. The Bible (ESV)/03. Leviticus/Leviticus 19\|Leviticus 19]])

"On these two commandments depend all the Law and the Prophets."
