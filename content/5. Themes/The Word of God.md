# The Word of God

Scripture is the written word of God — breathed out by God through human authors, authoritative, and living. But "the Word of God" in Scripture refers to more than a text: it is God's active, creative, revelatory speech — ultimately embodied in Jesus Christ.

## Key Passages

### God's Word as Creative and Active
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/01 Genesis/Chapter_01\|Genesis 1]] — God speaks the world into existence ("And God said…")
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/19 Psalms/Chapter_33\|Psalm 33]] — "By the word of the Lord the heavens were made"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/23 Isaiah/Chapter_55\|Isaiah 55]] — "My word… shall not return to me empty"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/58 Hebrews/Chapter_04\|Hebrews 4]] — "The word of God is living and active, sharper than any two-edged sword"

### The Law and Scriptures as God's Word
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/05 Deuteronomy/Chapter_08\|Deuteronomy 8]] — "Man does not live by bread alone, but by every word that comes from God"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/19 Psalms/Chapter_19\|Psalm 19]] — The Law is perfect, sure, right, pure, clean, true, and more desirable than gold
- [[Chapter_119\|Psalm 119]] — 176 verses meditating on every aspect of God's word
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/20 Proverbs/Chapter_30\|Proverbs 30]] — "Every word of God proves true; he is a shield to those who take refuge in him"

### Prophetic Word
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/24 Jeremiah/Chapter_01\|Jeremiah 1]] — "The word of the Lord came to me"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/26 Ezekiel/Chapter_03\|Ezekiel 3]] — Ezekiel is to eat the scroll and speak God's words
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/30 Amos/Chapter_08\|Amos 8]] — A famine not of bread but of hearing the words of the Lord

### Christ as the Living Word
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/43 John/Chapter_01\|John 1]] — "In the beginning was the Word… and the Word became flesh"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/58 Hebrews/Chapter_01\|Hebrews 1]] — God has spoken in these last days by his Son

### The Scriptures' Authority and Sufficiency
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40 Matthew/Chapter_05\|Matthew 5]] — "Not an iota, not a dot, will pass from the Law"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/40 Matthew/Chapter_04\|Matthew 4]] — Jesus rebukes temptation with Scripture ("It is written…")
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/42 Luke/Chapter_24\|Luke 24]] — Jesus opens the disciples' minds to understand the Scriptures
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/43 John/Chapter_17\|John 17]] — "Sanctify them in the truth; your word is truth"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/45 Romans/Chapter_10\|Romans 10]] — "Faith comes from hearing, and hearing through the word of Christ"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/55 II Timothy/Chapter_03\|2 Timothy 3]] — "All Scripture is breathed out by God and profitable for teaching, reproof, correction, and training in righteousness"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/61 II Peter/Chapter_01\|2 Peter 1]] — Prophecy "carried along by the Holy Spirit"; the prophetic word made more sure
