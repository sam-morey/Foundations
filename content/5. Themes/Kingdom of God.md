# Kingdom of God

The Kingdom of God is God's sovereign rule — his reign over creation, over Israel, and ultimately over all nations. In the NT, Jesus announces that the Kingdom has arrived in him, though its full consummation remains future.

## Key Passages

### God as King in the Old Testament
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/02 Exodus/Chapter_15\|Exodus 15]] — "The Lord will reign forever and ever"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/19 Psalms/Chapter_47\|Psalm 47]] — God is King over all the earth
- [[Chapter_93\|Psalm 93]] — The Lord reigns
- [[Chapter_96\|Psalm 96]] — Declare his glory; the Lord comes to judge
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/23 Isaiah/Chapter_52\|Isaiah 52]] — "Your God reigns!" (the gospel announced)

### Israel's Request for a King
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/09 I Samuel/Chapter_08\|1 Samuel 8]] — Israel asks for a king; God grants their request
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/10 II Samuel/Chapter_07\|2 Samuel 7]] — God establishes his eternal kingdom through David

### The Coming Kingdom
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/27 Daniel/Chapter_02\|Daniel 2]] — A stone not cut by human hands fills the earth
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/27 Daniel/Chapter_07\|Daniel 7]] — The Son of Man receives an everlasting dominion

### The Kingdom and the Cross
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/43 John/Chapter_18\|John 18]] — "My kingdom is not of this world"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/42 Luke/Chapter_23\|Luke 23]] — "Remember me when you come into your kingdom"

### The Kingdom in the Letters
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/45 Romans/Chapter_14\|Romans 14]] — The Kingdom of God is righteousness, peace, and joy in the Holy Spirit
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/46 I Corinthians/Chapter_15\|1 Corinthians 15]] — Christ must reign until all enemies are under his feet

### The Kingdom Consummated
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/66 Revelation of John/Chapter_11\|Revelation 11]] — "The kingdom of the world has become the kingdom of our Lord"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/66 Revelation of John/Chapter_20\|Revelation 20]] — The millennial reign
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/66 Revelation of John/Chapter_21\|Revelation 21–22]] — The new creation as the eternal kingdom

## Already / Not Yet
The Kingdom has already arrived in Jesus (his healings, exorcisms, and resurrection), but is not yet fully consummated. Believers live in the overlap of the ages, called to embody Kingdom values now.
