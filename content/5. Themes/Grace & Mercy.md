# Grace & Mercy

Grace is God's undeserved favor — giving good things to those who deserve judgment. Mercy is withholding deserved punishment. Together they describe the character of God at the heart of the gospel.

## Key Passages

### Grace & Mercy in the Old Testament
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/02. Exodus/Exodus 34\|Exodus 34]] — God's self-revelation: "merciful and gracious, slow to anger, abounding in steadfast love"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/04. Numbers/Numbers 6\|Numbers 6]] — The Aaronic blessing: "The Lord be gracious to you"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/16. Nehemiah/Nehemiah 9\|Nehemiah 9]] — A prayer recounting God's repeated mercy despite Israel's faithlessness
- [[Psalms 103\|Psalm 103]] — "He does not deal with us according to our sins"
- [[Psalms 136\|Psalm 136]] — "His steadfast love endures forever" (refrain of every verse)
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/23. Isaiah/Isaiah 55\|Isaiah 55]] — "He will abundantly pardon"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/25. Lamentations/Lamentations 3\|Lamentations 3]] — "The steadfast love of the Lord never ceases; his mercies never come to an end"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/28. Hosea/Hosea 11\|Hosea 11]] — God's anguished love for rebellious Israel
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/32. Jonah/Jonah 4\|Jonah 4]] — Jonah angry that God shows mercy to Nineveh

### Grace & Mercy in the New Testament
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/43. John/John 1\|John 1]] — "Grace and truth came through Jesus Christ"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/42. Luke/Luke 15\|Luke 15]] — The Prodigal Son: a parable of the Father's extravagant grace
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/45. Romans/Romans 5\|Romans 5]] — "Where sin increased, grace abounded all the more"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/45. Romans/Romans 9\|Romans 9]] — "I will have mercy on whom I have mercy" — mercy is God's sovereign gift
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/49. Ephesians/Ephesians 2\|Ephesians 2]] — "By grace you have been saved through faith… it is the gift of God"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/56. Titus/Titus 3\|Titus 3]] — He saved us not because of works but because of his own mercy
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/58. Hebrews/Hebrews 4\|Hebrews 4]] — Come boldly to the throne of grace to find mercy and help in time of need

## Steadfast Love (*Hesed*)
The Hebrew word *hesed* — often translated "steadfast love," "loving-kindness," or "mercy" — is one of the OT's richest theological terms. It describes covenant loyalty that goes far beyond mere obligation.

Key *hesed* texts: [[2. The Bible (ESV)/02. Exodus/Exodus 34\|Exodus 34]], [[2. The Bible (ESV)/08. Ruth/Ruth 1\|Ruth 1]], [[Psalms 103\|Psalm 103]], [[2. The Bible (ESV)/33. Micah/Micah 6\|Micah 6:8]], [[2. The Bible (ESV)/25. Lamentations/Lamentations 3\|Lamentations 3]]
