# Grace & Mercy

Grace is God's undeserved favor — giving good things to those who deserve judgment. Mercy is withholding deserved punishment. Together they describe the character of God at the heart of the gospel.

## Key Passages

### Grace & Mercy in the Old Testament
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/02 Exodus/Chapter_34\|Exodus 34]] — God's self-revelation: "merciful and gracious, slow to anger, abounding in steadfast love"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/04 Numbers/Chapter_06\|Numbers 6]] — The Aaronic blessing: "The Lord be gracious to you"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/16 Nehemiah/Chapter_09\|Nehemiah 9]] — A prayer recounting God's repeated mercy despite Israel's faithlessness
- [[Chapter_103\|Psalm 103]] — "He does not deal with us according to our sins"
- [[Chapter_136\|Psalm 136]] — "His steadfast love endures forever" (refrain of every verse)
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/23 Isaiah/Chapter_55\|Isaiah 55]] — "He will abundantly pardon"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/25 Lamentations/Chapter_03\|Lamentations 3]] — "The steadfast love of the Lord never ceases; his mercies never come to an end"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/28 Hosea/Chapter_11\|Hosea 11]] — God's anguished love for rebellious Israel
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/32 Jonah/Chapter_04\|Jonah 4]] — Jonah angry that God shows mercy to Nineveh

### Grace & Mercy in the New Testament
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/43 John/Chapter_01\|John 1]] — "Grace and truth came through Jesus Christ"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/42 Luke/Chapter_15\|Luke 15]] — The Prodigal Son: a parable of the Father's extravagant grace
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/45 Romans/Chapter_05\|Romans 5]] — "Where sin increased, grace abounded all the more"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/45 Romans/Chapter_09\|Romans 9]] — "I will have mercy on whom I have mercy" — mercy is God's sovereign gift
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/49 Ephesians/Chapter_02\|Ephesians 2]] — "By grace you have been saved through faith… it is the gift of God"
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/56 Titus/Chapter_03\|Titus 3]] — He saved us not because of works but because of his own mercy
- [[2. The Bible (ESV)/58 Hebrews/Chapter_04\|Hebrews 4]] — Come boldly to the throne of grace to find mercy and help in time of need

## Steadfast Love (*Hesed*)
The Hebrew word *hesed* — often translated "steadfast love," "loving-kindness," or "mercy" — is one of the OT's richest theological terms. It describes covenant loyalty that goes far beyond mere obligation.

Key *hesed* texts: [[2. The Bible (ESV)/02 Exodus/Chapter_34\|Exodus 34]], [[2. The Bible (ESV)/08 Ruth/Chapter_01\|Ruth 1]], [[Chapter_103\|Psalm 103]], [[2. The Bible (ESV)/33 Micah/Chapter_06\|Micah 6:8]], [[2. The Bible (ESV)/25 Lamentations/Chapter_03\|Lamentations 3]]
